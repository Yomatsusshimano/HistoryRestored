# Roopkund: the legacy radiocarbon citation

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). C031/R006, S411–S413. No independent review. [Structured controls](../data/roopkund-legacy-date-controls.json).

The [2023 pathology chapter](https://www.heritageuniversityofkerala.com/Publications/P1/42_Barthwal%20and%20Walimbe_PPJ_20230131.pdf), PDF3/printed807, reports a skeletal-material date of **650 ± 150 years**, citing Crane and Griffin1958. This page was rendered and visually inspected. It supplies no sample number, reference epoch, confidence convention, calibration or pretreatment. Preserve the reported number without assigning CE bounds or adding a sample row to the modern dating ledger.

The [PubMed catalog](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17835848/) identifies *University of Michigan Radiocarbon Dates III*, H.R. Crane and J.B. Griffin, Science128(3332):1117–1123, 7November1958, DOI10.1126/science.128.3332.1117. Catalog metadata does not authenticate its Roopkund entry. The chapter says Michigan State University, whereas the cited article title says University of Michigan. This bibliographic discrepancy requires the original entry; it is not evidence of deliberate rewriting.

The [1963 ListVIII publisher extract](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/radiocarbon/article/university-of-michigan-radiocarbon-dates-viii/AF78FD21EE562A0CA115390C29143075) provides a relevant laboratory-method control. Its authors say earlier Michigan lists enlarged counting errors by at least a factor of two to allow other process errors. ListVIII instead reports counting standard deviations, with a1950 zero and5568-year half-life. This establishes a change of convention; it does not establish the exact1958 Roopkund uncertainty or its confidence level. Do not halve150 or transfer ListVIII's epoch/half-life to ListIII without inspecting it. The publisher's2016 online date is distinct from the original1963 publication.

## Retrieval limits and next test

The1958 original was not acquired from the checked Science/JSTOR routes. An institution-hosted Negi scan linked by a secondary lead article timed out; its edition and contents remain unverified. No access control was bypassed. Only the ListVIII extract, not its full PDF, was inspected.

Retrieve ListIII's introduction and exact Roopkund entry, then establish laboratory ID, material, submission/collection context, preparation and error convention. Recover any accession crosswalk before comparing it with2019 bones or2023 skull numbers. Until then, the legacy result neither contradicts nor confirms a particular modern sample. The [37-sample comparison](ROOPKUND-DATING-AUDIT.md) is unchanged: bone ages test mortality hypotheses, not the age of later redistribution or a mud layer. All twenty objectives remain active.
