# Shelbyville Main Street: 1886, 1893 and 1933 map sequence

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). C030/R004; S388–S390. No independent review. [Structured inspection and comparison ledger](../data/shelbyville-map-sequence.json).

Original maps now support a comparison of the **block** bounded by Main, Morgan, Washington and South First streets. They do not yet authenticate the individual modern 212/216 parcel, the surviving vault or the Reddit photograph. This distinction matters because mapped footprints and numbering change across editions.

## Stable block topology, changing footprints

The same four bounding streets and an internal north–south alley allow a block-level comparison. The block is labeled 4 in the selected maps. All three selected frontages retain **W. Main** west of Washington, while current business/report identifiers say **E Main**. No dated renaming or renumbering instrument has been acquired.

The 1886 and 1893 keys explicitly distinguish actual street numbers from arbitrary consecutive numbers. Thus the earlier frontage labels **47–56** must not be silently read as modern civic addresses. The 1933 frontage instead carries **1826 through 1810**, with separate numbers along Washington. The map sequence demonstrates different displayed numbering schemes, without yet establishing the legal crosswalk or date of the change to modern 200-series identifiers.

These are selected readings of original drawings, with positions matched by streets and alley. They are not survey-grade parcel registration, independently established construction/demolition events or assertions that the current structure corresponds to any one entry. The 1893 **being built** annotation is contemporary map evidence of an unfinished construction description; it supplies no contractor, exact work date or completion certificate. Different use labels can describe occupancy changes without fabric replacement. Different footprint/material/story depictions warrant a records comparison, but do not identify the process by themselves.

## Why this does not measure burial

The early map keys mark circled numbers as relative heights. Their datum, units and measurement lineage remain unverified in this audit; no height values have been adopted or subtracted. Building-story counts are a separate notation. A change from a one-story yellow footprint to a two-story red one in the corresponding block position cannot be converted into ground-level rise without demonstrating the same fabric, parcel and elevation reference.

The drawings record substantial building-layout context over decades. They do not supply an inspected sediment section, original-grade elevation at the vault, natural-deposit age or thickness. They also do not independently verify the 2020 engineer report's uncited historical grade-buildup account. Equal scrutiny requires dated grade records for that account as well as physical evidence for a flood account.
