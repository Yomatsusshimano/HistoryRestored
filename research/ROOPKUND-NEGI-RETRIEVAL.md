# Roopkund: recovered Negi account and dating controls

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). C031/R006, S414. No independent review. [Retrieval ledger](../data/roopkund-negi-controls.json).

The [institution-hosted PDF](https://ignca.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/NKBose007b.pdf) is R.S. Negi's *Rupkund Human Remains: A Mystery Yet Unresolved*. PDF1/2/4/8 were visually inspected. PDF2 discusses the 2013 pilgrimage postponement, so this edition cannot be a contemporaneous 1956 report. Its publication date remains unknown; a URL directory is not a publication certificate.

PDF4 reports two wooden samples dated at Calcutta and London: 470 ± 120 and 500 ± 60 years. It separately reports Lucknow skeletal material sent to Michigan, with 650 ± 150 years. PDF8/reference3 points to Science128:1122 through Sax1991, and reference4 identifies a1959 government mimeograph. These remain retrieval targets, not inspected originals. No specimen identifiers or full measurement conventions are supplied in this passage.

## An arithmetic check, not a calendar conversion

Taking the wooden numbers literally as symmetric intervals gives [350,590] and [440,560], whose intersection is **[440,560]**. The account's stated overlap [500,590] does not follow from that arithmetic. Neither calculation is a calibrated calendar interval, joint confidence result or established death date. The discrepancy does not identify its cause.

Wood growth, artifact manufacture/use, death and later sediment deposition require separate associations. Without original laboratory entries, sample context and a common dating convention, these reports cannot become a simultaneous-mortality test. They do not date a mud deposit.
