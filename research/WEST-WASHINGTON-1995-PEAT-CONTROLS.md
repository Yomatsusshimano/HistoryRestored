# Washington peat: later boring controls

Research draft, 2026-10-11. S442; no independent scientific review.

The [public assessment packet](https://www.a2gov.org/media/xw5f1typ/415-washington_phase-i_2013.pdf) reproduces NTH's March29,1996 corrective action plan at PDF371 and individual1995logs at PDF425/426. The selected intervals are transcribed in [the dataset](../data/west-washington-1995-peat-controls.json). Upper material includes paving/concrete and fill with construction debris; the complete profiles have not been transcribed. Reported total boring depths are30ft and20ft, respectively.

A fill designation is an investigator's interpretation, not an independently dated emplacement history. An interval without that designation is not automatically authenticated natural peat. These differing labels require examining original field notes, exact positions and disturbance history before using peat as a dating target. Organic material age, emplacement age and flood age must remain distinct.

The logs provide elevation numbers, but the datum and benchmark are not verified. No conversion toNAVD88 or correlation with the earlier wells is adopted. Sample identifiers for dating and physical sample availability remain unknown. No radiocarbon determination, event age or undisturbed horizon is established.

Navigation through PDF350–429 located later reports and these logs; it did not locate the corrected1992MW-1/2/3 sheets. That is a scoped search result, not proof they are absent from the complete packet or archives. PDF344's water-level table also marksMW-2/3 not surveyed; casing/water levels must not be substituted for missing ground elevations.
