# Younger regional constraints: Bullhead Alluvium

2026-10-08. Selected-source audit; no original age recalculation or field verification.

S208, [Howard et al.2015](https://pages.uoregon.edu/rdorsey/Downloads/HowardEtal2015.pdf), describes Bullhead Alluvium inset into eroded Bouse and Hualapai deposits. Its age discussion on PDF p.12 was visually inspected. This identifies potentially useful younger constraints, but their sites and correlation steps must remain explicit.

|4.41 ±0.03 Ma Sandy Point basalt | Greggs Basin; lava within60 m of river gravel | Different locality; original basalt analysis and local-to-regional unit transfer pending |

The inset deposit is younger despite lying at a lower present elevation. Elevation alone is not stratigraphic order. If the tephra formed in place in a demonstrably younger deposit and its correlation age is valid, it supplies a younger chronological limit on the eroded older unit. Reworked ancient ash would require a different interpretation. The original descriptions and sample context must decide this, rather than assuming either primary deposition or redeposition.

These controls are stronger leads for an age bracket than another rock beneath Bouse. They do not yet establish the age of the Hart Mine Wash DCL or individual fossil samples: a measured connection from that target section to the dated northern succession remains missing in this archive. Neither the numerical value nor the shared Bullhead name can substitute for that connection.

S208's approximate4.5–3.5 Ma main aggradation span is explicitly an interpretation within broader constraints. It is not one measurement with a confidence interval. The paper also combines magnetic, sedimentological and regional correlation arguments; those dependencies require separate checks.

## Later challenge to the regional chronology

S209, [Crow et al.2021](https://digitalcommons.usu.edu/geology_facpub/583/), DOI10.1130/G48080.1, abstract accessed through the institutional repository, proposes river arrival at the proto-Gulf between4.80 and4.63 Ma, instead of the earlier two-arrival model. It attributes the discrepancy probably to repeated section across faults and uses sanidine dating plus magnetostratigraphy. The downloadable full text returned403 through web access. These are authors' conclusions from an inspected abstract, not an independently reproduced chronology or verified fault correction.

Retain this later alternative when reviewing the S2012018 regional model. A revised ancient regional chronology is not automatically a historical chronology, and neither publication wins by date or authority. Compare the same measured sections, fault relationships and dated samples.

Next recover House2008b tephra sample/log records, Matmon2012 burial inputs and Crow2021 full methods/supplement. Prioritize the Nomlaki bed's primary-deposition evidence and the erosional sequence before transferring its age to a specific Bouse horizon.
