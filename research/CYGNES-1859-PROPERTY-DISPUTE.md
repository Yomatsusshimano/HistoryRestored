# An 1859 property dispute and channel-filling authorization

Research draft, 2026-10-11. S361; C006. No independent review.

The [15 April 1859 Gazette des tribunaux](https://data.decalog.net/enap1/Liens/Gazette/18590415.pdf), issue **9997**, carries a report headed *Propriété de l'île des Cygnes — Revendication de l'État contre la Ville de Paris*. The original first-page masthead and complete island article across columns 2–3 were visually inspected, with higher-resolution selected crops. PDF page 1 is the locator; no printed page number is invented. The remaining three PDF pages were not visually inspected. [Acquisition and claim ledger](../data/cygnes-1859-property-dispute.json).

This is a contemporary source for the reported nineteenth-century litigation and a retrospective newspaper account of earlier legislation and works. It is not the original 1773 letters patent, a certified judgment, a contractor's account or an independently dated sediment record. The issue predates the reported 1887 tower foundation works and supplies a documentary explanation that was circulating before that construction.

## Authorization is not completion

The report's opening says a Seine branch bordering the former island was filled in **1773**. Later it describes letters patent dated **20 June 1773** as authorizing the City to fill the small branch, form streets, sell a portion to the École Militaire and sell the remaining island land and the channel **to be filled**. The opening's completed-action wording and the later authorization/future-work wording must stay separate. Neither identifies the dates or quantity of executed filling. A 1773 completion date is not accepted from the introductory sentence alone.

The same article reports a sale to the École Militaire by acts of **26 and 30 September 1773**, for **62,100 livres**. This is a reported transaction and a precise archival retrieval lead, not inspected original sale instruments or proof of payment. Its mixed historical land-area units are not converted into modern area here.

The planned archive palace, university-related buildings and Beaux-Arts facilities are not automatically completed structures. The account says construction was begun and interrupted in 1814, then reproduces a letter about the started archive-palace works losing their original purpose. No material quantities or foundation locations are authenticated by this passage.

## The official account was contested

The article describes competing positions: the State treated the City as holding the land for a prescribed public use rather than as owner; the City defended its title. It reports a Conseil d'État opinion dated **6 January 1858** supporting the State's legal position, followed by a civil-court outcome rejecting the State's claim and recognizing City ownership. The heading lists March hearings but does not authenticate an exact judgment date here.

This demonstrates a reported institutional disagreement over legal title. It does not establish that the channel or terrain history was fabricated. The reported opinion and court disposition must be distinguished from the earlier grants and from physical ownership/use evidence. Original case papers, judgment and the reproduced legal texts remain unacquired.
