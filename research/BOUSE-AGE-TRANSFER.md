# Which Bouse age constraints test historical deposition?

2026-10-08. Synthesis of inspected records S194–S207; no new dating, field verification or independent review.

The current archive has recovered analytical tables and documented sample identities. It has not established the depositional age of each fossil-bearing bed. These are different achievements. Environmental interpretation, age and a continuous Gulf-to-Pacific route remain separate tests.

## Direction of a constraint matters

Let A be age before present, so larger A means older. For an intact sequence with dated primary ash below a target bed, A(target) ≤ A(ash). That older limit alone permits recent deposition. For a dated primary ash above a target bed, A(target) ≥ A(ash), which can exclude historical deposition if the overlying ash is ancient and the relationship is secure. Numerical uncertainties and systematic errors must propagate into either inequality. No probability is assigned here.

A dated crystal in reworked sediment supplies a constraint on the crystal's history; it does not fix the sediment's deposition time. A demonstrated primary fallout layer dates that layer much more directly, but the elapsed time to an adjacent bed still requires evidence. Primary deposition at one outcrop does not establish simultaneous deposition throughout a formation.

## Consequential next evidence

Choose a target bed by section and sample ID before making an age claim. For the Hart Mine Wash environmental transition, recover an explicit measured connection to independently dated material above and below that bed, including unconformities, reworking and sample-position uncertainty. An upper ancient primary horizon would constrain the question in a way that another underlying age cannot.

For Amboy, first match the analyzed ash sample to the basal versus potentially reworked sublayer. Then recover the interval-C sampling heights and any independently dated overlying unit. A seemingly continuous section is not a measured short lag without sedimentary and chronological support.

For BP2/C27, obtain original analytical results rather than assigning BP1's age by resemblance alone. The O’Connell section61/62 sample labels are retrieval anchors, not completed dating. No source search or later observation can be counted as a prospective prediction when its target was already inspected.

These are evidence requirements for testing H2-T, not findings that the beds are young. Unresolved transfer does not outweigh the original studies by default or establish a historical catastrophe. Conversely, the existence of an ancient source mineral cannot be used to dismiss a later-deposition hypothesis without testing the proposed redeposition and contacts. Any historical alternative must specify positive transport, preservation and timing evidence.

## Effect on the wider reconstruction

The RD-16 revision is documented calculation history, and the9.2 Ma basalt/tuff discrepancy is a traceable material-description error. Neither establishes intentional rewriting. Resolving them improves the evidence inventory without supplying a common global event date.

No Bouse result by itself establishes H2-G, the northern connection depicted on an island map. That claim still requires a fixed route, compatible terrain/deformation and dated in-place deposits along the entire route. A local marine episode and an island-surrounding passage are geographically different propositions.
