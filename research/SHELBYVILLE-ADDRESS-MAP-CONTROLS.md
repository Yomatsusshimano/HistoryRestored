# Shelbyville: paired-address lead and original 1886 map controls

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). C030/R004, S387–S388. No independent review. Follows the [engineer-report audit](SHELBYVILLE-VAULT-ENGINEER-REPORT.md). [Structured controls](../data/shelbyville-address-map-controls.json).

## A possible explanation for 212 versus 216

S387 is the [Realty.com listing for 212 & 216 Main Street](https://www.realty.com/commercial-listings/342317688/212-216-Main-Street-Shelbyville-IL-62565), MLS 11718228, attributed to Matt Forcum/Shelby Realty Services. Its description associates Sit n Sip with two adjoining buildings and reports separate basement, ground-level and street-level entrances for each. It gives a listing date of 10 February 2023. Selected rendered text was inspected; property photographs, original MLS submission, survey and deeds were not inspected.

This is a plausible bridge between the engineer report's 212 address and the administrative business record's 216 address. It remains an interested commercial description distributed through a listing platform, not an authenticated parcel survey or proof that both records concern the same vault. Repeated listings sharing that description would not be independent confirmation. A separately found Shelby Realty page returned only a loading shell in the inspected web response and supplied no additional substantive verification.

The platform also displays **Year Built 1701**, without a supporting record. Preserve that as an unverified field, not a construction date or evidence of an earlier civilization. Its automatic map/nearby-place information was not adopted for geographic registration. Neither the listing's floor labels nor its date establishes the original function or construction age of the lower level.

## Original map recovery

S388 is the Library of Congress [March 1886 Sanborn map of Shelbyville, Shelby County, Illinois](https://www.loc.gov/item/sanborn02153_001/), two sheets. The institutional item JSON and both advertised full JP2 files were acquired by normal HTTPS. Both complete sheets were visually surveyed as reduced overviews; full-resolution crops of sheet1's key and the Main Street frontage between Morgan and Washington were inspected. The remainder was not exhaustively transcribed. The item metadata and printed sheet titles agree on March 1886 and Illinois, preventing confusion with other Shelbyvilles.

The printed key distinguishes **actual** street numbers from **arbitrary consecutive** ones. It identifies story-number and window conventions, building-material colors and circled numbers indicating **relative heights**. These features are explicit source controls. They do not supply a verified modern address crosswalk, a vertical datum or an independently established height unit in the inspected portion. No circled height value has been converted into a ground elevation or a burial-depth estimate.

On sheet1 the relevant Morgan–Washington frontage is drawn along a segment labeled **W. Main**. The modern business/report identifiers say **E Main**. Preserve the map's literal label while investigating the numbering and directional history; do not silently substitute a modern label. Commercial footprints and uses appear along that frontage, but no individual 1886 footprint has yet been authenticated as the current 212/216 structure. A footprint's presence would establish mapped fabric at that date only after a defensible location match; it would not by itself establish that the surviving walls, vault or floor level are unchanged.

## What these records change

The combined-address listing makes the apparent address conflict more tractable. The original map provides a contemporary building-layout and relative-height source for a historical comparison. Neither is yet a verified grade-change measurement. Next acquire the original parcel/address crosswalk, inspect later Sanborn sheets for continuity and numbering changes, and recover street-grade ordinances or engineering profiles with dates and units. Compare those with the same vault and a dated soil profile before selecting a natural or engineered burial explanation.

The engineer report's grade-history background remains an uncited assertion pending these records. Its February 2020 filling recommendations remain separate from proof of execution. Original Reddit photograph, measured deposit thickness, deposit age and common event association remain unresolved. All twenty original objectives remain active.
