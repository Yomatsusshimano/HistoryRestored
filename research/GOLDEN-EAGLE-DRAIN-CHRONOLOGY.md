# Golden Eagle drains and chronology constraints

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York), S459 / Sacramento R008. Selected original report pages inspected; no independent scientific review.

The [Golden Eagle report](https://asc.sonoma.edu/historical-archaeology-golden-eagle-site) supplies an ordered drain/pit reconstruction, but its calendar assignments combine artifact bounds, historical occupation and presumed flood correlations. These must remain separate. This extends the [section audit](GOLDEN-EAGLE-SECTION-CONTROLS.md).

## Spatial and physical controls

Plan1, printed78/PDF110, places Features8/14 in AreaVII near TrenchH and Features16/27 immediately south in AreaIX. Section-reference points are depicted. California Street is at the plan's north edge, K Street south and Seventh Street east. The key calls datum points above sea level, but the survey's connection to an independently authenticated vertical reference remains unverified. No map coordinates or sea-level measurements are derived here.

Plate5, printed71/PDF103, shows the half-sectioned Feature8 looking south. Figure2, printed72/PDF104, is explicitly an idealized, unscaled reconstruction: early fill90 outside the later pit, Layer70 above it, later fills81-85 within the recut pit and a redwood plank. It cannot be treated as a measured field section or an independent observation confirming Section16.

Section15, printed99/PDF131, contains north/south profiles of the AreaIX drain complex with Layer70 and the drain fills79/80 labeled. Its relationships should be read with the accompanying prose, not assigned calendar years directly from the drawing. Section10, printed92/PDF124, is a contractor-exposed north section south of TrenchE; it is an actual drawing, unlike the omitted Section9. No full layer/elevation transcription of Section10 is claimed here.

On printed74 the report also infers removal of3-4ft of soil to explain subsoil hollows, contrasting them with exposed flat ground elsewhere. This is a proposed excavation history; it is not a measured global burial thickness. The specific drainage function and connection to the city sewer remain conjectural in the author's account. Repeated historical flood language within one report does not multiply independent dated horizons.

The resulting testable local sequence is earlier drain/pit activity, a presumed flood-silt horizon, later recutting/lining, sealing and refuse disposal. The1861-62 identification remains conditional pending independent sediment characterization and age controls. It supports investigating multiple phases rather than pooling all buried material into one episode. No Reddit photograph join, global event, inherited civilization or deliberate chronology rewriting is demonstrated.
