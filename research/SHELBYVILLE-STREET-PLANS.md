# Shelbyville: original street plans, datum and repair scope

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). C030/R004; S403–S405. No independent review. [Retrieval and selected-record ledger](../data/shelbyville-street-plans.json).

The original IDOT plan set identifies the Sit-N-Sip frontage and local street-work controls. It explicitly uses an **assumed datum**, so it does not yet connect modern design heights to the [1907 USGS courthouse height](SHELBYVILLE-ELEVATION-CONTROLS.md). A reproduced 2019 email also proposes deliberate vault filling. Neither source dates the original vault construction or establishes natural sediment deposition.

## Original plans and provenance

The [IDOT contract record](https://webapps1.dot.illinois.gov/WCTB/LbContractDetail/Index/68d7f804-00a5-4e39-a8d0-12a1df42d84d?contractId=371f2289-dd3e-402a-b359-f6772e689bb0&page=2) identifies contract **95864**, section **13-00055-00-SW**, project **TU34(077)** and a **January 17, 2020** letting. Its advertised directory links the [44-sheet plan scan](https://apps.dot.illinois.gov/eplan/desenv/011720/150-95864/PLANS/PL-95864-150.pdf) and [92-page special-provisions packet](https://apps.dot.illinois.gov/eplan/desenv/011720/150-95864/95864-150.pdf). These are proposed contract documents, not a final inspection or an as-built survey. Catalog pay quantities and bidder information are not evidence of actual material placed.

The plan cover names **S. Alan Spesard / Shelby Engineering** as preparer, with a September 14, 2019 preparation date and November 18, 2019 state approval entries. Selected internal roadway sheets carry September 6, 2019 dates and The Upchurch Group title blocks. These roles and dates are retained separately; they do not become the building's construction date. Cover station limits are 1050+71.69 to 1058+34.00, stated length 762.31 feet.

All 44 sheets were rendered for a thumbnail contact sheet used only to select pages. Detailed visual inspection covered sheets **1, 2, 6, 18, 19, 20, 23, 30 and 31**. Most plan pages yielded no extracted text; no claim of a complete searchable or detailed visual review is made.

## Local benchmarks and frontage

Sheet 2 states that all plan elevations are based on an assumed datum. Sheet 6 lists four hydrant benchmarks:

| Plan mark | Described location/point | Listed elevation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | “M” in Mueller on hydrant, northwest corner of West Washington/Main | 648.39 |
| B | “M” in Mueller on hydrant, northwest corner of Morgan/Main | 644.14 |
| C | Northmost rimbolt on hydrant, southeast corner of Broadway/Main | 653.10 |
| D | Northmost rimbolt on hydrant, northeast corner of Walnut/Main | 646.48 |

The plan uses feet for its dimensional scale; these elevation numerals are preserved on the plan's assumed datum. None is the unstamped aluminum tablet on the courthouse's second step. A baseline tie sketch uses the corner of the courthouse's bottom step as a landmark, not as a recovered vertical benchmark. Visual proximity or similar numbers cannot establish a datum tie.

Sheet 20 labels two **SIT-N-SIP** frontages on the south side of Main, east of Morgan and west of an alley. Sheet 23 supplies grading details over that area. This strengthens the frontage identification, but the inspected sheets do not give the modern 212/216 parcel numbers or the vault-floor/undisturbed-soil surface needed for a burial calculation. No floor-height difference is adopted as flood thickness.

Sheets 30–31 distinguish storm-sewer profiles from surface grades. Pipe inverts, proposed profiles and existing profiles are different measurements. They must not be silently substituted for original street level or a natural depositional boundary.

## Provisions distinguish types of vault and work

Special-provisions **PDF page 20 / printed page 10** was visually inspected. Its domestic-meter-vault item concerns water service boxes adjusted to proposed grades; that term does not identify the occupied building's brick sidewalk vault. The sidewalk item specifies new concrete at least five inches thick, a four-inch fine-aggregate bed and matching existing business-wall/door elevations with stated step/ramp exceptions. These are specified works, not measurements of a historic flood layer. No completion, exact local execution or actual fill volume is inferred.

## April 2019 vault-fill proposal

The third-party-hosted [City Hall correspondence packet](https://edgarcountywatchdogs.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/To-City-Hall-part-2.pdf) has 193 PDF pages. **PDF page 36 / printed email page 1** was rendered and visually inspected. It reproduces a Shelby County Highway email dated **April 10, 2019, 7:29 AM**, proposing a Motor Fuel Tax program that includes flowable fill for concrete vaults beneath Main Street sidewalks. It asks for agenda placement and describes the program as revisable. It signs “alan”; identity is not independently authenticated or expanded from that signature alone.

The email names an attachment containing estimates, a resolution and an engineering agreement; that attachment was not identified and inspected in this batch. No approval, placement, volume or parcel-specific execution is inferred. Original municipal mailbox provenance remains unverified. The highlighted reproduction is not republished here, nor is unrelated correspondence or contact information.

## Remaining test

Recover the survey's assumed-datum origin and field level book, then establish stable ties to historical marks and the target frontage. Obtain the municipal vault-work contract, approval, placement diary, material tickets and final/as-built records. Original construction and dated sediment contexts remain separate questions. These plans make those tests more specific; they do not establish a catastrophe, inherited construction or chronology fabrication. All twenty objectives remain active.
