# Danville: an acknowledged identification change and conditional age transfer

2026-10-09. C005/S327. Selected author-uploaded main text, printed420-421, inspected; no original main scan or independent review.

The [2021 study](https://doi.org/10.1130/2021.1217(16)), in its Danville discussion, explicitly acknowledges an early misidentification of the Danville tuff southwest of Mount Diablo as Nomlaki. It attributes that identification to the senior author's communication to Isaacson around1990 and says later chemical, mineralogical, isotopic-age and stratigraphic evidence separated the units. This is a documented revision of a local identification. It does not automatically explain the different [FLV-119-WW group assignments](ARTISTS-DRIVE-2008-CROSSWALK.md).

## The stated reasoning and its dependencies

The text describes Danville as a biotite-bearing glass-rich ash in northern Livermore Valley. It correlates that ash with a layer near the base of the marine Ohlson Ranch Formation near Plantation. The authors note microprobe differences but judge the neutron-activation analyses sufficiently similar to correlate the two localities. The numerical Table2analyses have not been independently reproduced in this follow-up; that judgment is retained as the authors' interpretation.

For the Ohlson Ranch locality, the discussion reports dates attributed to McLaughlin et al.2012b:

| Material / analyzed region | Method | Reported age, Ma | Reported uncertainty convention |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Zircon interiors | U-Pb |4.58±0.30|2sigma|
| Zircon interiors | U-Pb |4.36±0.11|2sigma|
| Zircon rims | U-Pb |4.367±0.042|2sigma|
| Biotite | Ar-Ar |4.9±0.8|2sigma|

These are the2021authors' reports of another study's measurements, not newly inspected assays. The original2012abstract, full analytical data, sample identifiers, zircon selection and calibration remain unacquired. Interiors and rims are not counted as independent eruption events. The2021text says the2012authors preferred the younger zircon estimates.

The authors further infer that Danville overlies the Lawlor Tuff through lateral projection within the Sycamore Formation. They explicitly say the two layers do not occur together at one site. Thus the stated order depends on a projected correlation, not a locally exposed two-bed contact. The point estimate4.9Ma is older than their quoted Lawlor point age, but that point ordering alone is not a statistical rejection of the broad biotite measurement. No uncertainty model or stratigraphic likelihood is fitted here.

The discussion also considers the Den Hartog Tuff chemically similar to Danville and reports an older5.0±0.3Ma glass K-Ar result. Its cautions about glass behaving as an open system are the authors' explanation, not an independently established diagnosis of that specimen. The age uncertainty convention for this quoted glass result is not specified here.

## Preserve different age labels instead of choosing silently

The main discussion uses a heading approximately4.6-4.4Ma and presents a combined estimate4.57±0.4Ma, while also regarding the younger zircon dates as compatible with the inferred sequence. Its averaging recipe, weights, covariance and uncertainty convention are not reproduced. The earlier retained supplemental group label approximately4.8Ma(4.37Ma) remains a distinct source statement. Neither label is a direct date of FLV119.

No selected passage explicitly joins the acknowledged Mount Diablo misidentification to the2008Willow Wash row or documents the rationale for assigning the light-coloured Willow Wash fraction to Danville. The1991description of two FLV119populations, the2008mean vector, and the2021fraction record therefore remain separate evidentiary steps. A general history of mistaken identification cannot fill the missing sample-specific decision record.

Next inspect the original neutron-activation analyses for the Danville/Ohlson reference samples and the2012dated-sample report, then seek the explicit FLV119component decision. Only after that can chemical identity, eruption age and primary deposition be tested for a named regional bed. This identification revision supplies no historical catastrophe date or fabrication mechanism.
