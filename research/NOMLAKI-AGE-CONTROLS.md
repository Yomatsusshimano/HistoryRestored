# Nomlaki age construction and the younger Bullhead constraint

2026-10-09. C005/S325; selected original-source audit, without independent review or field verification.

The original [House, Pearthree and Perkins 2008 paper](https://wpg.forestry.oregonstate.edu/sites/default/files/seminars/08_Houseetal_GSA_SP.pdf), GSA Special Paper439, pp335-353, DOI10.1130/2008.2439(15), supplies the methods behind two controls cited in the [Bullhead assessment](BULLHEAD-YOUNGER-CONSTRAINTS.md). PDF pages1/8/11/17 (printed335/342/345/351) were visually inspected. Other pages were text-searched, not visually audited. The full copyrighted PDF is retained locally, not redistributed here.

## What the numbers measure

| Tephra | Reported estimate | Construction described on printed351 | What has not been reproduced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Informal lower Nomlaki |4.06 ±0.46 Ma, explicitly1sigma; rounded4.1 ±0.5 Ma | Weighted mean of two linear extrapolation estimates: Bristol Lake core CAES#1 and Artist Drive section2 | Original age-depth inputs, individual extrapolated ages, weights and the empirical error-model implementation |
| Nomlaki Tuff |3.29 ±0.05 Ma | Weighted average of two extrapolation estimates: Burmester, Utah, core and Willow Wash, California, section; uses the preceding error model | Original controls, extrapolation inputs, weights and error propagation; this passage does not separately restate the uncertainty's sigma convention |

These are correlated tephra age estimates derived from other sections, rather than newly measured local argon ages. The empirical error model is attributed to M. Perkins,2007personal communication, and described as reflecting sedimentation-rate variation across Basin and Range sections/cores. Two contributing estimates and later citations of the same result are not wholly independent clocks. Extrapolation is an assumption-bearing dating method; its use alone neither invalidates the estimate nor supplies a historical age.

The authors report lower Nomlaki reversed polarity and two possible chron assignments, calling for a better age estimate. Those polarity statements cite other studies; this page does not provide a new local polarity measurement. The authors also acknowledge glass-composition variation exceeding analytical error and the possibility of multiple similar tephras, while provisionally treating five samples as one layer. Their correlation uncertainty must remain visible. This audit does not independently reproduce their glass analyses or polarity chronology.

## The local order matters separately

Printed342 describes lower Nomlaki exposures in tributary fan gravel at365-390m above sea level, with nearby Bullhead alluvium in stratigraphically higher positions up to400m. Printed345Figure10 provides a photograph and interpreted section at the Powerline outcrop, showing lower Nomlaki, Black Mountain fanglomerate and Bullhead strata. The profile also identifies surface gravel lags; those cannot silently substitute for intact overlying beds. A contact interpretation drawn on a published photograph is not new field verification.

Printed342 describes thin, west-dipping piedmont gravels above an erosion surface cut on Bullhead alluvium and Black Mountain fanglomerate. Nomlaki Tuff is intercalated in these later deposits at395m and350m. The authors infer at least50m of incision by approximately3.3Ma. This is their regional sequence interpretation, not an independently measured displacement field or an event duration. Lower present elevation does not make the later fan stratigraphically older.

For an age in years before present, a secure primary ash in a deposit demonstrably younger than the eroded Bullhead would imply **older Bullhead age >= ash age**. But if old ash was redeposited much later, its eruption age would not date that later deposition. The inspected passages report an intercalated ash layer but do not supply a complete sample-level primary-versus-reworked test. Neither explanation should be assumed from the numerical age alone.

## Consequence for the historical-map hypothesis

The source strengthens the documented northern relative sequence and makes the age dependencies explicit. A historical assignment to this sequence would need to explain or revise the correlation, extrapolation controls and contact evidence together. No revised historical age is established here.

This does not transfer a numerical age to the Hart Mine Wash fossil-bearing target in southern Bouse. The original northern section must be connected to that exact target through measured contacts and defensible correlations. Nor do these ages establish a continuous historical Gulf-to-Pacific passage. Environmental evidence, chronology and route geometry remain separate requirements.

Next recover the four original extrapolation sections' age-depth controls and the error-model recipe; inspect sample-specific ash texture/contacts for primary deposition; then trace a measured northern-to-target stratigraphic connection. A repeated regional age citation cannot close those gaps. See [Bouse age-transfer rules](BOUSE-AGE-TRANSFER.md).
