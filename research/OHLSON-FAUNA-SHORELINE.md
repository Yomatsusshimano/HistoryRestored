## Original ecological assessment

The [letter in the institutional dissertation](https://thesis.caltech.edu/238/13/Prentice_cs_1989.pdf), printed249, names Ohlson samples87-OR-51A,87-OR-51B and87-OR-54. Ingle describes an arranged, numbered slide for51A and scatter mounts for51B/54. Those preparations are retrieval identifiers, not measured depths or proof that all specimens came from one horizon. The letter compares these samples with86-PAOR-1, previously examined material from Point Arena; that identifier is distinct from the dated zircon86-FR-1HP.

All identified species were reported still living on the Pacific coast. Ingle therefore does not regard the faunas as a definitive biostratigraphic age determination. He considers their warm/cool composition suggestive of middle-to-late Pliocene conditions. The environmental interpretation is inner-to-middle shelf, approximately30-60m water depth, supported by comparison with a modern Sonoma shelf study. Neither an age likelihood nor depth uncertainty is supplied. Page250 proposes analyzing Quaternary terrace samples to test the faunal distinctions; it does not report completion of that test.

Environmental resemblance supports a marine interpretation but does not supply an exact calendar date. Conversely, living taxa do not prove recent deposition: survivorship alone cannot select an age. The letter's sample identities have not been connected to the dated ash by a measured section, nor independently taxonomically reexamined.

## What the shoreline drawing actually represents

Figure4-3 distinguishes preserved formation patches, an approximate shoreline, a projected continuation and a separate Point Arena marine deposit. Its caption derives shoreline location from three interpreted surface classes: deposits preserved, deposits removed, and deeply weathered surfaces regarded as never inundated. The Point Arena offset shoreline is postulated. The diagram is not an independently surveyed contemporaneous coastal chart.

Printed173 explicitly treats lithologic and fossil agreement as permitting a correlation, without establishing it. The proposed50km offset requires both that correlation and interpretation of the ridge's weathered surfaces. Thus the mapped line, shared fauna and ash age cannot be counted as three independent confirmations of one dated shoreline.

## Consequences for reconstruction

Retain marine-environment support separately from numerical chronology and geographic correlation. Do not add the interpreted30-60m depth to the later reported350m platform elevation to claim measured uplift: their sample/location/datum joins, sea-level history and uncertainty are unverified. No continuous interbasin passage or rapid global displacement follows from this local reconstruction.

Next retrieve the named slides and their field-section records, original modern ecological calibration (Quinterno and Gardner1987, cited in the letter; full paper not acquired), and independent contacts/dates for the Point Arena comparison. The search for the original2012ash dating report still yields later citations rather than its analytical data; no absent identifier or inaccessible abstract is treated as negative geological evidence.
