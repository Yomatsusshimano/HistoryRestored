# What the lamp case can distinguish

Retrospective assessment, 2026-10-08. These comparison versions operationalize H5/H7; they are not claims attributed verbatim to Nathan, prospective predictions or an exhaustive partition of possible histories. All cited cases remain SOURCED_DRAFT, without independent review.

The inspected lamp records support specific nineteenth-century disclosures, design revisions and reported experiments by several named participants. They do not establish an ancient device or a recovery pathway. That result narrows this selected case; it does not estimate how often inventions elsewhere involved borrowing, appropriation or recovery.

H5-D and partial borrowing can coexist. A recovered component could also undergo genuine experimentation. The notebook evidence therefore challenges a version denying the recorded developmental activity; it does not logically exclude every possible undiscovered antecedent. Conversely, keeping recovery logically possible is not positive support for it.

## Evidence paths, not a count of agreeing sources

1. **Antecedent disclosure:** the [Woodward/Evans comparison](LAMP-PATENT-COMPARISON.md), S18/S130/S132, distinguishes patent, register and drawing dates. It supports earlier named technical work. It does not establish later access, commercial performance or lost-civilization provenance.
2. **Draft revision:** [S175/S176](LAMP-DRAFT-TO-PATENT.md) show six manuscript claims versus five granted claims with corresponding apparatus. The missing standalone salt-coating claim is observed; why it changed requires prosecution records. Repeated performance language is not a replicated trial.
3. **Reported experiment:** [S177/S178](LAMP-TRIAL-RECORDS.md) record carbon-thread preparation, readings and failures. The 13.5-hour stage and subsequent higher-output hour cannot be compressed into a standardized lifetime. The manuscript dates and S179's editorial correction remain separate.
4. **Calculation and measurement dependence:** [S180-S182](UPTON-LAMP-CALCULATIONS.md) add voltage entries, arithmetic and adjacent failures. Upton and Batchelor may describe the same test. Within Upton's calculations, resistance appears derived from voltage ratios; apparent numerical agreement is not an extra independent measurement. Neither notebook calibrates the other's instruments.
5. **Ownership and retrospective testimony:** the [socket judgment](LAMP-PATENT-COMPARISON.md#adversarial-testing-the-1894-socket-judgment), S56, addresses selected claims and summarizes Bergmann's account of factory adoption. A court's summary is neither the original factory order nor an independent witness to its execution. S174's university-hosted Woodward biography expressly credits Wikipedia and cannot count as an independent purchase record.

## Fair attribution at the present evidence level

The public record should identify Woodward and Evans for the inspected earlier disclosure, Edison for the attributed draft and named patents, Batchelor for the inspected experimental notes, Upton for the inspected observations/calculations, and Johnson and Bergmann for their distinct socket disclosures. These are scoped documentary roles, not a complete list of contributors or a final ranking of invention credit. An archive author field alone cannot establish who physically performed every step.

No named person should be replaced with an inferred lost-civilization inventor without positive evidence. Equally, later corporate ownership or a famous inventor's name should not absorb other documented participants. This is an actionable correction to overly compressed storytelling, even while the broader recovery hypothesis remains unestablished.

## Next work with the highest discriminating value

Resolve an actual transfer instrument and trace a specific subsequent credit claim; this can test H7-T directly. Recover instrument/circuit descriptions and unique specimen links before pooling notebook measurements. For H5-R, identify a concrete antecedent candidate rather than treating missing modern paperwork as the antecedent. A further similar notebook entry would enlarge the archive, but would add little independent weight unless it resolves one of these questions.

No global chronology conversion, catastrophe-to-recovery sequence, prevalence estimate or independently validated museum account follows from this case. The original twenty objectives remain open where their required evidence is absent.
