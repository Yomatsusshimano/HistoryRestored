# Golden Eagle: distinguish flood silt, fill and refuse

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). Sacramento R008 follow-up, S459. No independent review.

Selected original pages of the [1980 Golden Eagle report](https://asc.sonoma.edu/historical-archaeology-golden-eagle-site) provide a local sequence with several deposition processes. They do not describe one uniform burial deposit. The J/K/6/7 excavation block remains separate from the unverified Reddit J/11th photograph. [Structured layer controls](../data/golden-eagle-deposit-controls.json) preserve reported observations, interpretations and missing dates.

These classifications are the author's conclusions, not independently tested sediment identifications. The similar-looking silt layers are not automatically the same event. Modern refuse is reported in Feature 21/2 and Layer 16; photographs, accession records and original sections still need to be joined to the prose. No independent natural-deposit age is assigned here.

## What the dates mean

Layer 24's ceramic registry mark supplies a reported 1849 earliest-possible deposition constraint, not an 1849 construction date (printed61/PDF93). Feature 6's saloon association combines artifact constraints with occupational history; Feature 8's later-fill interval depends principally on documents (printed64-65/PDF96-97; printed70/PDF102). Those are not independent absolute sediment dates.

Layer identifiers record encounter order, not chronology. The excavation used a local grid and temporary manhole benchmark; its connection to a recognized vertical datum remains unverified (printed54/PDF86). Do not read a numbered layer or reported elevation as an event date or evidence of uplift.

## Limits that affect reconstruction

Feature 8's earlier and later materials were bagged together during part of recovery; Feature 15's screen mesh changed mid-excavation (printed56/PDF88). These limit phase-specific artifact and biological comparisons. Modern disturbance varied strongly across the site.

Two printed conversions conflict: Trench E overburden is1-2 ft but parenthetically 0.9-1.8 m (printed50/PDF82); Area I trench length is 15 ft but 5.2 m (printed59/PDF91). Both literal pairs are retained. Mathematical conversions are 0.3048-0.6096 m and 4.572 m, respectively; **these calculations do not establish which reported measurement is correct**. No corrected field value is adopted.

The next discriminating audit is the original sections 9/14/16, associated artifact marks and dating chapters, and independent sediment characterization. Flood silt may coexist with later street raising, refuse disposal and demolition fill. This site does not establish global synchrony, an inherited civilization or a fabricated chronology.
