# Allen Creek: plan custody and physical-model input limits

Research draft,2026-10-11. S440; no independent scientific review.

The [2012Flood Insurance Study](https://www.a2gov.org/media/lutb3224/flood-insurance-study-washtenaw-co-4-3-2012-vol-1.pdf), volume1, effectiveApril3,2012, identifies county-held1925plans byR.A.Dodge (PDF27/printed22). It also identifies1993McNamee,PorterSeeleyexisting-condition/repairplans. This is a report that the study authors obtained those records, not direct inspection of the original plans or confirmation of when construction was completed.

PDF27/28, printed22/23, describes surveyed terrain and a computational representation of underground drainage. The equivalent rectangular model channel was adjusted to represent the arch sewer's hydraulic conveyance. **A model cross section is not a natural soil section.**

The study models divided overland flow around the railroad between riverstations3184–4288, fromWestHuron towardCatherine. Station coordinates and input files remain unacquired. A modeled flow path is neither an observed1902deposit nor a dating result.

The terrain descriptions include five-foot contour sources with interpolated one-foot contours and, in another passage,30-meterDEMs. Survey months also differ. The exact reach-to-input crosswalk needs verification; interpolated spacing does not establish measurement accuracy.

PDF31/printed26 usesNAVD88 and reports a countyaverage conversion of minus0.4feet fromNGVD29. **That numerical change is a reference-system conversion, not observed ground upheaval.** It cannot supply the missing elevations of the earlier well logs or authenticate a historic benchmark's datum.
