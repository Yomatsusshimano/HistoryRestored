# Plate II: detailed shaft labels and the prose mismatch

Research draft, 2026-10-11. S358; C006. No independent review.

The original [plate II](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/zoom/8976363) belongs to the separately catalogued [Planches volume](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/8976346) of Gustave Eiffel's *La tour de trois cents mètres*, held by ETH-Bibliothek Zürich, Rar 9704, [persistent identifier](https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-28271), Public Domain Mark. Full 4000-pixel delivery was visually inspected, with section/shaft crops and a 6000-pixel delivery crop of pier 1 for numerical readings. Only this plate was inspected; the manifest is navigation metadata, not an inspected atlas. Images are linked rather than mirrored. [Acquisition and numerical ledger](../data/eiffel-foundation-plate-II.json).

## What the drawing supplies

Figures 1 and 2 are geological sections along the axes of piers 1–2 and 4–3, referring to figure 5 of plate I. Figure 3 shows investigation shafts for pier 1 (north) and pier 4 (west). Other figures provide excavation plans and sections for the four piers. The plate distinguishes interpreted geological correlations, shaft observations and construction layouts. A continuous geological boundary between shafts is not continuous sampling.

Figures 1 and 2 print horizontal scale 0.0005 per metre and vertical scale 0.002 per metre: nominal vertical exaggeration **4**. Figure 3 prints 0.01 per metre. These are printed scales, not a calibrated pixel-to-ground conversion; scan distortion has not been measured. The earlier S357 bulletin profile's ratio **5** belongs to a different drawing. Their section lines have not been registered together.

## Pier 1: selected depth/elevation pairs

The detailed shaft labels give the following values. Labels describe source-reported geological materials, without independently determining their age or origin. The last row is the top of plastic clay; sampling termination and the clay's full thickness are not inferred from the bottom of the drawing.

All ten pairs satisfy **34.53 − depth = printed elevation** with exact decimal arithmetic. The consecutive depth intervals total 15.90 m. These checks establish transcription/arithmetic consistency of the selected labels, not field accuracy or independent geological validation. The 14.50 m boundary marks the reported end of the shaft and start of the auger, not a newly identified lithological contact.

The diagram subdivides the interval below the fill into sandy clay, several sand/gravel layers and limestone. It contains more detail than the [prose audit](EIFFEL-FOUNDATION-CONTEXT.md). The prose's grouped 7.43 + 5.99 + 3.37 m interpretation still totals 16.79 m, exceeding surface-to-clay difference by **0.89 m**. No exact correspondence of its grouped 5.99 m interval to the drawing has been established. The diagram therefore does not authorize replacing the prose value or declaring the cause a typographical error. Both readings remain visible.

## Pier 4 and unresolved location controls

Figure 3 explicitly distinguishes the pier-4 excavation performed before shaft sinking, approximately 6.60 m from the stated surface to the +28 platform. The shaft layout differs from pier 1. A complete numerical transcription of pier 4 is not part of this audit, and its earlier prose surface variants remain unresolved. It cannot be pooled with pier 1 as an identical investigation.

The plate names piers, not S357 boreholes 20, 22 and 24. Plate I's location plan, original Paulin/Arrault logs, modern survey datum, observation dates and exact object positions remain unjoined. No date for the fill or a common burial horizon follows from depth alone. All twenty objectives remain active. Next acquire plate I and register these section lines before attempting the bulletin-profile or historical-channel comparison.
