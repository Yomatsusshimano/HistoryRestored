These gaps are arithmetic consequences of the transcribed intervals, not confidence bounds on event duration. They challenge assigning the sloths' biological ages to the bat. Neither biological date locates a bone in a sediment unit or dates its later movement. A younger bat and older sloths could enter a deposit at different times or be mixed later; those possibilities require physical evidence.
