2026-10-08. Retrospective synthesis at the current public checkpoint. This assessment has no independent scientific or historical review. It preserves all twenty objectives in [the protocol](../PROTOCOL.md); it does not declare completion of the proposed reconstruction.

The archive establishes several local observations, documentary relationships and conditional numerical constraints. It has not established one worldwide event, a common chronology transformation, inherited civilization or a causal chain from catastrophe to fabricated history. The strongest presently testable conclusion concerns narrower versions: some nominated remains cannot be assigned to a short common mortality episode while retaining their reported dating sets. Allowing later redeposition changes the question and requires additional evidence.

## Constraints that a reconstruction must satisfy simultaneously

**Sediment emplacement.** The [Copalis vented-sand audit](COPALIS-VENTED-SAND.md) adds reported dikes, sills and craters alongside surface deposits, including observed vents with zero sheet thickness. If authenticated, these require subsurface emplacement to be explained; surface inundation alone is incomplete. The source itself leaves the trigger uncertain and treats deep-aquifer deformation speculatively. Coordinate/depth discrepancies and limiting plant-material ages prevent a unique reconstructed profile or exact event age. This regional uncertainty does not establish a worldwide flood, and the Ws episode must not be merged with Y-associated1700 subsidence without correlation and dating evidence.

**Timing and association.** Mortality, mineral deposition, tree growth, document signing and patent registration are different events. The [Camp Century audit](CAMP-CENTURY.md) dates sediment conditionally and documents separate plant/context limits. The [Canadian lamp audit](LAMP-PATENT-COMPARISON.md) distinguishes a July 24 drawing from an August 3 register entry. Neither distinction is itself a chronology break. Any proposed conversion must identify the exact event and timescale it transforms.

**Water and sediment budgets.** The [Missoula capacity check](MISSOULA-PULSE-ALTERNATIVE.md) shows that a flow capped at one million cubic metres per second passes no more than 8,640 cubic kilometres in 100 days. If all 100,000 cubic kilometres must pass that section, those premises cannot hold together. The source has not established the cap: this is a conditional test. Pulsing beneath the same cap cannot fix the deficit; changed discharge, routing, storage or released volume must be demonstrated. No executed global flow or sediment model exists here.

**Environment and geometry.** [Lake Cahuilla](CAHUILLA.md) supplies reported freshwater deposits and an inland-lake comparison. Water-bearing deposits alone do not demonstrate the marine strait implied by some island-map interpretations. A proposed former connection needs a specified route, elevations, salinity indicators and dated in-place units. The present map collection has not supplied that reconstruction.

The older [Bouse comparison](BOUSE-DISCRIMINATION.md) preserves genuine marine/lake disagreement. Same-genus isotope change survives, but within-sample spread and trace faunal exceptions prevent an idealized uniform transition. The living Streptochilus analogy concerns habitat flexibility within marine settings, not established freshwater tolerance. This local environmental question cannot stand in for the historical date and complete channel required by H2-T/H2-G.

**Construction and technical transmission.** The [Eiffel account](EIFFEL-CONSTRUCTION-AUDIT.md) names workers, departments and a supplier, and supplies internally consistent selected cost totals. The [1888 diagram, photograph and 1889 inspection](EIFFEL-VERTICALITY-REPORT.md) add reported and depicted intermediate erection states. Together they challenge an unchanged, already-complete structure, while leaving component manufacture and possible reuse as distinct questions. H4-I, H4-R and H4-N are retrospective operational comparisons introduced here, not claims attributed verbatim to the project owner or successful prospective predictions. Partial reuse could coexist with new manufacture; these versions do not exhaust every mixed history. [Lamp disclosures](LAMP-PATENT-COMPARISON.md) document multiple named contributions and distinct claim features. Earlier technology is not automatically recovered technology: a recovery hypothesis still needs a dated antecedent and an evidenced access route.

The [Bouse age-transfer assessment](BOUSE-AGE-TRANSFER.md) incorporates recovered analytical rows, sample locators and original age-control corrections. Underlying dates alone do not establish a target fossil-bed age; a secure ancient overlying horizon or bounded depositional lag would be more discriminating. RD-16's9.3-to9.6 Ma change is explicitly reported as recalculation in S207, not an unexplained chronological break. BP2/C27 and Hart Mine Wash transfer remain unresolved.

**Chronology revision versus fabrication.** [Campo Laborde](CAMPO-LABORDE.md) documents a specimen-specific chemical revision and earlier disclosed doubts. [Ice-core research](ICE-CORE-CHRONOLOGY.md) documents calendar corrections and stated unused checks. Both contain unresolved audit questions. Neither supplies a general date conversion or evidence of intentional historical rewriting. A local transcription or code error must be assessed for its actual consequences rather than promoted into a global mechanism.

## Colorado burial: what changes the cross-case inference

The [Colorado audit](COLORADO-BURIAL-DATING.md) adds 22 cobble measurements from a different physical dating method than mineral crystallization ages. It conditionally challenges **recent first burial**: the published interpretations are in millions of years. This remains a model-dependent result, not an independently reproduced age. All four sites have age-summary discrepancies; the printed decay equation has a sign/ratio-order inconsistency; and the original corrected Bayesian implementation remains unavailable. These issues warrant correction and sensitivity analysis. They do not establish young ages or deliberate fabrication.

Our [errors-in-both-isotope diagnostic](../data/burial-eiv-fit.json) reproduces approximate retained-sample slopes for some sites using a separately declared method. It does not reproduce the original age calculation. Restoring PVD021 changes Palo Verde's slope from 1.651889 to 1.424877, while the diagnostic Q/(n−2) changes from 1.011 to 1.038. That makes selection consequential without proving the restored cobble shares a burial history. Fits and error assumptions must be tested against physical shielding and provenance, not promoted into a unique chronology through numerical agreement alone.

**Later redeposition is a separate hypothesis.** A previously buried cobble could retain a signal older than its enclosing deposit. The source itself discusses reburial/exhumation possibilities at Palo Verde. Neither that possibility nor a fitted line supplies the date, footprint or mechanism of a recent event. A proposed common catastrophe must explain specimen-linked exposure histories and dated enclosing contacts across sites. This is the same association requirement applied to the wildlife cases; it is not a reason to discard inconvenient ages wholesale.

**Location agreement has a limited scope.** Bat Cave's reported coordinate lies in the section named for the 1973 west-bank photograph under both declared datum scenarios. Yet it lies about 87–107 m from mapped Trbb in the conditional geological overlay. A surface polygon can represent cover above a cut, so the mismatch does not prove a wrong sample assignment. The regional Qa2 thickness range of 1–10 m is not a local measurement at the reported 7.23 m sampling depth. Neither the surveyed southeast-quarter geometry nor the photograph-to-cut-to-bed crosswalk is established. Resolving that crosswalk would materially improve the burial test; another general regional map citation would not.

The older field accounts support investigating a **regional** boulder-flood mechanism. Their reported 22 km reach, separate younger terraces, mixed clast sources and unobserved direct stratigraphic relationships constrain it. They supply no demonstrated connection to Arctic fossils, urban fill, inherited buildings or historical rewriting. A regional catastrophic process and a worldwide historical claim require different evidence.

## Next decisions and their evidence requirements

1. **Test common later deposition directly.** Recover the missing Coyote Canyon mammoth assay documentation and specimen-to-bed linkage. Compare it with the sediment units and camel association already audited. Establish what the local evidence can discriminate before projecting a horizon elsewhere.
2. **Audit the most consequential dating inputs.** Camp Century's original methods supplement and all nineteen data workbooks have now been recovered; selected residual, fading and central-age calculations have been reproduced. Full aliquot fitting, aggregation, shared-error covariance and DRAC processing remain unresolved. Recover the sloth endpoint preparation records separately. Component calculations do not constitute reproduction of the pooled age or independent validation.
3. **Define a testable geographical reconstruction.** Prioritize a measured dated bracket for a named Bouse fossil bed over accumulating additional underlying ages; see [age-transfer requirements](BOUSE-AGE-TRANSFER.md). Specify one proposed former connection and a source/flow model with dated discovery observations. Only then freeze a prediction for uninspected observations. Existing audited cases cannot become held-out predictions retrospectively.
4. **Obtain independent criticism.** A reviewer can challenge an input, association, model assumption or inference using the linked records and scripts. Publication alone is not independent review, and no reviewer acceptance is claimed. External outreach still requires the project owner's authorization.
5. **Discriminate erection from manufacture.** Cross-identify Eiffel members in the progress records and part registers, then trace supplier dispatches and receipts. Seek positive antecedent evidence for reuse under the same provenance standards. The surviving finding aids identify leads and losses; they do not substitute for the original transactions.
6. **Trace a specific invention-credit change.** The [lamp synthesis](INVENTION-DISCRIMINATION.md) separates development, exclusive credit, ownership and ancient recovery. Seek the original transfer instrument and a dated later attribution change; do not count another derivative biography as independent evidence.
7. **Discriminate regional flood mechanisms before assigning years.** The [recording-sensitivity calculation](FLOOD-DISCRIMINATION.md) quantifies how much additional angular allowance would close each selected magnetic contrast. A common rigid rotation cannot do so; differential recording effects need measurements. Recover those observations alongside the actual 2003 reference input, retaining ash and annuality tests as separate constraints.

The current evidence does not justify teaching the starting hypothesis as established history. It does justify publishing documented local findings, reproducible limitations and corrections openly. Institutional adoption, worldwide archival replication and independent prospective discoveries remain outstanding outcomes, not consequences inferred from repository publication.
