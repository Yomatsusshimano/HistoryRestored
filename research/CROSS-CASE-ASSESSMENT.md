# What the audited cases currently establish

2026-10-08. Retrospective synthesis at the current public checkpoint. This assessment has no independent scientific or historical review. It preserves all twenty objectives in [the protocol](../PROTOCOL.md); it does not declare completion of the proposed reconstruction.

The archive establishes several local observations, documentary relationships and conditional numerical constraints. It has not established one worldwide event, a common chronology transformation, inherited civilization or a causal chain from catastrophe to fabricated history. The strongest presently testable conclusion concerns narrower versions: some nominated remains cannot be assigned to a short common mortality episode while retaining their reported dating sets. Allowing later redeposition changes the question and requires additional evidence.

Reported interval incompatibility is not a statistical probability of rejection. Likelihood penalties are conditional on the implemented error model. These retrospective examples were selected for investigation; they do not estimate the prevalence of anomalies in all geological or historical records.

## Constraints that a reconstruction must satisfy simultaneously

**Timing and association.** Mortality, mineral deposition, tree growth, document signing and patent registration are different events. The [Camp Century audit](CAMP-CENTURY.md) dates sediment conditionally and documents separate plant/context limits. The [Canadian lamp audit](LAMP-PATENT-COMPARISON.md) distinguishes a July 24 drawing from an August 3 register entry. Neither distinction is itself a chronology break. Any proposed conversion must identify the exact event and timescale it transforms.

**Water and sediment budgets.** The [Missoula capacity check](MISSOULA-PULSE-ALTERNATIVE.md) shows that a flow capped at one million cubic metres per second passes no more than 8,640 cubic kilometres in 100 days. If all 100,000 cubic kilometres must pass that section, those premises cannot hold together. The source has not established the cap: this is a conditional test. Pulsing beneath the same cap cannot fix the deficit; changed discharge, routing, storage or released volume must be demonstrated. No executed global flow or sediment model exists here.

**Environment and geometry.** [Lake Cahuilla](CAHUILLA.md) supplies reported freshwater deposits and an inland-lake comparison. Water-bearing deposits alone do not demonstrate the marine strait implied by some island-map interpretations. A proposed former connection needs a specified route, elevations, salinity indicators and dated in-place units. The present map collection has not supplied that reconstruction.

**Construction and technical transmission.** The [Eiffel account](EIFFEL-CONSTRUCTION-AUDIT.md) names workers, departments and a supplier, and supplies internally consistent selected cost totals. The [1888 diagram, photograph and 1889 inspection](EIFFEL-VERTICALITY-REPORT.md) add reported and depicted intermediate erection states. Together they challenge an unchanged, already-complete structure, while leaving component manufacture and possible reuse as distinct questions. H4-I, H4-R and H4-N are retrospective operational comparisons introduced here, not claims attributed verbatim to Nathan or successful prospective predictions. Partial reuse could coexist with new manufacture; these versions do not exhaust every mixed history. [Lamp disclosures](LAMP-PATENT-COMPARISON.md) document multiple named contributions and distinct claim features. Earlier technology is not automatically recovered technology: a recovery hypothesis still needs a dated antecedent and an evidenced access route.

**Chronology revision versus fabrication.** [Campo Laborde](CAMPO-LABORDE.md) documents a specimen-specific chemical revision and earlier disclosed doubts. [Ice-core research](ICE-CORE-CHRONOLOGY.md) documents calendar corrections and stated unused checks. Both contain unresolved audit questions. Neither supplies a general date conversion or evidence of intentional historical rewriting. A local transcription or code error must be assessed for its actual consequences rather than promoted into a global mechanism.

Dependence can be scientifically useful; it limits what counts as independent confirmation. No numerical “combined confidence” is assigned across these heterogeneous cases.

## Next decisions and their evidence requirements

1. **Test common later deposition directly.** Recover the missing Coyote Canyon mammoth assay documentation and specimen-to-bed linkage. Compare it with the sediment units and camel association already audited. Establish what the local evidence can discriminate before projecting a horizon elsewhere.
2. **Audit the most consequential dating inputs.** Camp Century's original methods supplement and all nineteen data workbooks have now been recovered; selected residual, fading and central-age calculations have been reproduced. Full aliquot fitting, aggregation, shared-error covariance and DRAC processing remain unresolved. Recover the sloth endpoint preparation records separately. Component calculations do not constitute reproduction of the pooled age or independent validation.
3. **Define a testable geographical reconstruction.** Specify one proposed former connection and a source/flow model with dated discovery observations. Only then freeze a prediction for uninspected observations. Existing audited cases cannot become held-out predictions retrospectively.
4. **Obtain independent criticism.** A reviewer can challenge an input, association, model assumption or inference using the linked records and scripts. Publication alone is not independent review, and no reviewer acceptance is claimed. External outreach still requires Nathan's authorization.
5. **Discriminate erection from manufacture.** Cross-identify Eiffel members in the progress records and part registers, then trace supplier dispatches and receipts. Seek positive antecedent evidence for reuse under the same provenance standards. The surviving finding aids identify leads and losses; they do not substitute for the original transactions.
6. **Discriminate regional flood mechanisms before assigning years.** The [recording-sensitivity calculation](FLOOD-DISCRIMINATION.md) quantifies how much additional angular allowance would close each selected magnetic contrast. A common rigid rotation cannot do so; differential recording effects need measurements. Recover those observations alongside the actual 2003 reference input, retaining ash and annuality tests as separate constraints.

The current evidence does not justify teaching the starting hypothesis as established history. It does justify publishing documented local findings, reproducible limitations and corrections openly. Institutional adoption, worldwide archival replication and independent prospective discoveries remain outstanding outcomes, not consequences inferred from repository publication.
