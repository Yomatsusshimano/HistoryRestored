# Shelbyville storefront: identity and repair-record leads

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). R004, C030, S377 and S382–S385. No independent review.

Reddit led to a named business, an independently documented address and contemporary municipal correspondence about a damaged vault. These are useful retrieval targets. They do not yet authenticate the circulating photograph, a burial depth, the original floor function or a catastrophe date. [Structured ledger](../data/shelbyville-storefront-controls.json).

## Identity and depth controls

The earlier [Reddit discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/interestingasfuck/comments/gxsuj1/) reports a storefront 11 feet below the shops. A [later discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/AlternativeHistory/comments/15evx9c/a_puzzle_for_scientists_was_found_in_shelbyville/) alleges that the city is 4–5 metres underground. These are different numerical claims with no inspected measurement, datum or authenticated common image. Do not average them or treat reposting as replication.

A selected commenter in the later discussion identifies the red/tan building as **Sit n Sip**, attributes exposure to sidewalk renovation and reports an accessible rear lower-level door and storage use. Their local knowledge and these site observations remain unverified. Their suggested flooding/fire explanations are hypotheses. They supplied a historic-tour brochure and a Street View link; the latter was not inspected in this batch.

The [Illinois Liquor Control Commission PDF](https://ilcc.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/ilcc/news/uc-press-releases/fy-2019/2019-10-09%20Shelby%20County.pdf), S384, lists **Sit n Sip, 216 E Main Street, Shelbyville, Illinois**. The original page was visually inspected. Its release header says **21 July 2023**, while its body describes an operation on **9 October 2019**. Keep these date roles separate; the URL's 2019 directory is not a publication-date certificate. Only the address and date roles are relevant here. This independent address record does not establish that the Reddit image shows that parcel.

## Contemporary vault correspondence

S385 is a [publicly hosted 96-page scan of municipal correspondence](https://edgarcountywatchdogs.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/To-Mayor-part-1.pdf). The delivered file was acquired completely, searched for navigation, and original PDF pages **6, 68 and 69** were rendered and visually inspected. Pages 7 and 70 were inspected as extracted text only. The rest of the packet was not comprehensively read. This is a third-party copy of apparent contemporary records, not authenticated original email exports or an independently obtained city file. Highlights already appear in the delivered scan; they are not additional evidence.

On PDF69, a **12 February 2020** message under the subject Sit N Sip describes a partial collapse of a vault wall and an engineer's inspection/report expected that day. PDF68 contains a **13 February** message reporting that an attached engineer's evaluation recommended prompt repair, together with discussion of putting an emergency repair resolution before the council. The engineer's report and adopted resolution have not been inspected. The messages disagree in their prospective Monday/Tuesday meeting references; an actual meeting date or vote is not inferred from them.

These records support a known vault and a reported structural problem at the named business in February 2020. They do not identify a buried street, provide the vault's construction date, give a measured depth, or establish the cause of its original below-grade position.

**A separate address must stay separate.** PDF6 contains messages dated **5 and 8 June 2020** under the subject **222 Main St.**, discussing planned flowable-fill pours and asking for daily diary entries of filling dates and quantities. PDF7's extracted text discusses block-wall construction and permission to proceed. These are instructions and plans, not acquired completion diaries or measured fill volumes. They must not be reassigned to Sit n Sip at 216 E Main Street merely because the sites are nearby or appear in one packet. They do identify actual diary, contract and engineering-record targets for a local comparison.

## Historic photographs: floor loss does not specify burial

S383 is the two-page [CityWalk historic downtown brochure](https://www.shelbyvilleillinois.net/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/HDnew.pdf). Both original pages were visually inspected. It credits photographs to Donna Lupton/SCHGS and funding to Shelbyville Industrial Development. Its publication date remains unknown; the hosting path is not adopted as the date.

PDF1's Main Street panel describes a photograph looking west in the 1860s and foreground buildings destroyed by fire. It then describes an early-1900s eastward view and notes that many formerly three-story buildings now have two stories. PDF2's entry D, **200 E Main Street**, explicitly attributes loss of a third floor to a **1927 fire**. These are source-reported historical interpretations, without original photograph or fire-record authentication in this batch. Loss of an upper floor can change apparent building height without burying its lower floor. Neither the general streetscape statement nor the 200-address fire history establishes the history of 216 E Main Street.

## What would distinguish the explanations

Obtain the original photograph and its capture date, then match its facade and excavation to a specific parcel. Retrieve the Sit n Sip engineer's evaluation, council resolution and streetscape site records. Compare dated basement/vault plans, rear and front access elevations, historic street grades and soil profiles. Keep designed basement access, later deliberate filling, demolition debris, subsidence and natural deposition as separate testable explanations until the same fabric and deposit are linked to dated evidence.

No commenter or third party was contacted. No original image, sediment sample, deposit age, measured thickness or execution diary was acquired. All twenty original objectives remain active.
