# Partial channel filling and archival citation boundaries

Research draft, 2026-10-11. S362; C006. No independent review.

[Olivier Zunz’s 1970 study](https://www.persee.fr/doc/ahess_0395-2649_1970_num_25_4_422340), Annales25(4), pp1024–1065, DOI10.3406/ahess.1970.422340, was visually inspected on pages1024–1027 and1030. [Inspection ledger](../data/cygnes-zunz-archival-leads.json).

On p1025, the quoted20June1773authorization describes prior partial channel filling, flow chiefly during considerable floods, and summer stagnation. Zunz infers natural filling from map shape, current and activities; that inference is not an independently dated sediment result.

Footnote1, **A.N., E2013**, attaches to the1720grant and boat/timber use. Footnote2, **A.N., Q1 1281**, attaches to the1773École Militaire sale. No separate footnote beside the authorization quotation identifies its exact archival item. These are retrieval leads, not inspected original instruments.

Page1026 is an author redrawing after Roussel1730; page1027 reproduces a Carte des chasses labeled1764–1807. Neither supplies an authenticated single survey date or a foundation registration here. Page1028 has a large public-delivery mask and1029 is blank; neither supports underlying map/content claims. Other pages remain uninspected. Public advertised images were used; no PDF challenge or illustration restriction was bypassed, and images are not republished.

The [1859 report](CYGNES-1859-PROPERTY-DISPUTE.md) separately uses completed-action wording in its introduction and authorization/future-work wording later. The new quotation makes a staged sequence worth testing: partial infilling before authorization, followed by proposed completion. It does not establish when, how much or exactly where subsequent work occurred. Independence of the two accounts’ underlying legal sources is unverified.

Retrieve the sale and grant under the reported references, independently locate the authorization, and connect execution accounts and parcel plans to tower shafts. Actual completion date, material chronology and a common catastrophe horizon remain unknown. All twenty objectives remain active.

## Continuation inspected on 2026-10-11

Page1030 attributes final filling to **1812**, alongside the second quai d’Orsay section. This is Zunz’s historical claim, not a verified execution date; no separate footnote accompanies that sentence. Footnote1, **A.S., D1 Z, vol. 88**, follows the authorization’s land-use provisions. Footnote2, **Dictionnaire Lazare, ed.1855**, follows the resumed-quay/1704-council-act sentence. Their originals remain unacquired.

The source therefore describes a staged change rather than completion in1773. Keep1812 as a reported candidate, with actual completion unverified, until works records and parcel plans establish the sequence. No sediment age, universal burial horizon or exact foundation association follows.
