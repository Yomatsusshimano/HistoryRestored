# Ann Arbor: a candidate building and documented renovation

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). Reddit lead R005, S378/S416–S417. No independent review. [Structured controls](../data/ann-arbor-schlenker-controls.json).

The [Reddit discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/CulturalLayer/comments/excnvf/) names two different properties: a Starbucks on West Liberty and a Schlenker building farther along the street. The selected indexed comment proposes a debris flow from the northeast. Full thread and original photographs remain unavailable. Naming a nearby building does not authenticate either pictured parcel.

## Records acquired

The city's [1999 Historic District Commission awards](https://www.a2gov.org/media/l5gpckmi/1999-awards.pdf), PDF4, identifies the Schlenker hardware façade at **213 W. Liberty**. It assigns the brick store to1906 and reports restoration after an earlier porcelain-enamel front. That is a municipal account of work, not an original construction permit or sediment investigation. The selected page was rendered and visually inspected.

The Downtown Development Authority's [2003 plan](https://www.a2dda.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/RENEWAL_PLAN_2003-33-FINAL-091503-1.pdf), PDF1/33, was also visually inspected. TableA4 reports a **1998 renovation/addition**, **3,000 square feet**, at **213–215 W. Liberty**. The cover identifies the2003 amendment's adoption. The table measures building area; it gives no mud thickness, excavation volume or original ground elevation. The complete77-page delivery is hashed, but only these two pages were visually inspected.

## What this changes

R005 now has a specific candidate parcel and a documented alteration period to investigate. The original Starbucks photograph remains a separate identity problem. The record of façade restoration supplies an ordinary mechanism for changed front appearance; it does not explain the side openings without drawings or photographs.

The two institutional accounts may share underlying municipal records. Their agreement about location is not independent confirmation of construction age or any flood. The source-reported1906 date remains unverified against contemporary records. A business operating in the nineteenth century need not occupy the same surviving fabric throughout its history.

Retrieve the1998 permits, façade plans, original building survey, historical address crosswalk and dated side-elevation photographs. Compare original floor levels with surveyed street elevations and any in-place sediment profile on the same parcel. A proposed northeast debris flow must also fit terrain, sediment provenance and a dated depositional boundary; window arrangement alone cannot establish it. No event date or natural mud layer has been adopted. All twenty objectives remain active.
