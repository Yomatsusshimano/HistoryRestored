The [extractor](../analysis/ufvp_specimen_links.py) scans all554765 occurrence records and1967 multimedia records. All18targets match uniquely. The archive hash and download version are preserved; selected data attributed to Florida Museum of Natural History under its CC BY-NC4.0 license.

This advances the link from a paper's museum identifier to an actual published collection record. It does not independently authenticate the physical bone, laboratory subsample or original field entry. The export and papers may inherit the same collection documentation.

Preserve it alongside the2000DMS and2015bat labels, without treating rounded digits as accuracy or selecting a corrected findspot. Sharing a locality code or collection date does not establish a shared depth, sediment unit, death episode or deposition event.

The2000account reports June3, June8 and July4 work, with July4mapping only. The catalog contains other reported collection dates, including July3,7and9. UF73946holotype and the two dated Rhizoplagiodontia records carry July3. The discrepancy could involve collection, sorting, recording or transcription; none is established. Original bag labels and field notes are needed. Dates here are modern collection metadata, never the fossils' biological ages.

MKL998/1006/992 are now concrete retrieval codes. They have not been decoded as stratigraphic coordinates or bag depths. The export supplies no excavation-depth/contact field. All18target rows have blank otherCatalogNumbers, datum and coordinate-uncertainty cells; none has a matching multimedia row. That bounded result does not prove laboratory cross-references, survey data or specimen images are absent elsewhere.
