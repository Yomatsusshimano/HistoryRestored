# Plate I: pier layout and a mismatched figure reference

Research draft, 2026-10-11. S358; C006. No independent review.

The original [plate I](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/zoom/8976362) was acquired from the ETH-Bibliothek Zürich copy of Eiffel's 1900 *Planches*, Rar 9704, [persistent identifier](https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-28271), Public Domain Mark. The full 4000-pixel image and 6000-pixel crops of the plan and horizontal diagrams were visually inspected. No institutional image is mirrored publicly; [acquisition and controls](../data/eiffel-foundation-plan-I.json). This expands S358's selected atlas inspection, not the number of independent sources.

## The actual location plan

The exhibition location plan is explicitly **figure 6**, titled *Exposition de 1889 (Côté de la Seine), Tour de 300 mètres*, with printed scale **0.00025 per metre**. It shows the Seine, Pont d'Iéna and the exhibition's central axis, with Avenue de la Bourdonnais and Avenue de Suffren flanking the grounds. The plan labels all four piers:

| Pier | Printed directional label | Position within the plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Nord | Nearer the Seine, left of central axis on the sheet |
| 4 | Ouest | Nearer the Seine, right of central axis on the sheet |
| 2 | Est | Farther inland, left of central axis on the sheet |
| 3 | Sud | Farther inland, right of central axis on the sheet |

These labels are pier designations, not a north-up map convention. The flow arrow points toward sheet-right. No modern azimuth, coordinates, pixel-to-ground calibration or metric offset is derived from this inspection.

Together with plate II's named sections, this establishes a relative layout: the section through piers 1–2 occupies one side of the central axis, and the section through 4–3 the other. Piers 1/4 are the river-side investigations in S17's prose and plate II's shaft figure. This is correspondence within the same participant publication, not independent proof of field locations. The exact shaft centres and section endpoints are not digitized here.

## Printed reference versus observed figure

[Plate II](EIFFEL-FOUNDATION-PLATE-II.md), figures 1 and 2, directs the reader to **figure 5 of plate I**. But plate I's **figure 5** is a horizontal structural diagram of the upper tower, covering panels 18–29. The location plan is **figure 6**. This is a mismatch in the inspected original plates, not an OCR-only difference.

The plan's four pier labels make it relevant for the geological sections, but the original cross-reference is not silently rewritten. Whether the mismatch originated in preparation, engraving, an earlier version or another stage is unknown. No motive or historical-rewriting mechanism is inferred.

## What remains unconnected
