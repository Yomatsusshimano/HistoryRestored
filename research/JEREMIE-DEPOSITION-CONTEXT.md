The later dates must not be assigned to either layer merely because the taxon appears there. A regional date envelope also cannot date these units.

## Excavation and geometry

The registry names D. and T. Cordier and specific1984work dates, with July4for mapping only. Approximately50large bags of matrix were removed for later sorting. The cave is described as wet and without indications of past or present owl roosting. That observation neither proves all bones accumulated by falling nor excludes earlier predator contributions; the authors' general sinkhole-trap interpretation needs its own evidence. The owl-pellet interpretation at the separate2026Pozimán Cadena site must not be transferred here.

Reported shaft depth16.5m, opening2.5m and base width12m describe cave geometry. **Shaft depth is not sediment thickness.** No16.5m mud deposit, excavation volume or required flood flux can be computed from these dimensions. The0–25cm table scope likewise does not establish the full sediment depth or a mapped flood layer.

Page4says some associated skeletons were collected as units and that ambiguous associations were treated cautiously. This general collection policy does not authenticate the dated accessions' association or establish that all their elements represent single animals.

## Source limits that change the next test

The prose describes an abundance calculation from the upper5cm and includes a monkey percentage, while Table4lists the monkey only in the5–25cmred clay unit. The reason is unresolved; neither location can be assigned to a particular primate from these summaries. Original count sheets and bag labels could resolve the difference.

The registry's locality coordinates differ from the rounded labels in the2018supplement. Datum and survey accuracy are not recovered. These labels supply a locality lead, not demonstrated movement of the cave or precise animal findspots.

The2022article cites the2000account for geometry and faunal abundance. Agreement between those reports is transmission of the same evidence, not two independently measured sites. Unit differences also do not by themselves demonstrate two floods, continuous accumulation or catastrophic mixing.

A shared later event remains an unestablished hypothesis. The decisive next records are the1984map, excavation notes, bag labels and accession-to-unit links, followed by sample-linked sediment dating and contact/taphonomic analysis. This is concrete provenance progress toward the deposition test; it does not supply a shared catastrophe date, extinction mechanism or lost geography. All twenty original objectives remain active.
