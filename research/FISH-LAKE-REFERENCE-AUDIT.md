# Fish Lake reference chronology: what is independently checked?

Research draft, 2026-10-08. C008. Source-dependency audit, not a reproduced chronology.

The [Touchet study](TOUCHET-MAGNETIC-AUDIT.md) fits a combined Fish Lake, Oregon–Mono Lake, California magnetic reference curve. The original 1986 paper and assay table remain unrecovered here; publisher retrieval returned an error. A subsequent [NOAA archive recovery](NOAA-LAKE-RECOVERY.md) supplies numerical directions, but its Fish Lake record stops before the overlap described in 2003. Do not substitute data from the different Fish Lake in Utah.

[Hagstrum and Champion (2002), S170](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2001JB000524), provides an original volcanic-rock comparison and describes the reference's construction:

- Paragraphs 6 and 18 attribute Fish Lake age control to 18 radiocarbon dates and describe overlapping, internally correlated cores. These are reports of the earlier work, not recovered laboratory certificates.
- Paragraph 19 states that Fish Lake's overall declination was aligned using Mazama-associated lava directions also included in the comparison dataset.
- Paragraphs 11–16 describe adjustments to some volcanic ages based on magnetic ordering, averaging and smoothing. Table 1 retains adjusted ages separately; Table 2 includes interpolated curve positions.
- Paragraph 20 reports an approximately 280-year offset over the last 3,500 radiocarbon years, alongside poorer older agreement and sparse older volcanic coverage.

The text and table notes were inspected; figures and raw measurements were not. Neither curve fitting nor the reported offset has been reproduced here.

## Consequences for this investigation

There are several different questions of independence. Separate specimens can supply separate magnetic measurements while sharing an orientation anchor. Independently measured radiocarbon ages can still be adjusted using the magnetic pattern when constructing a curve. An interpolated point is a model output, not another dated specimen. Each relationship belongs in the evidence chain.

Agreement around the shared Mazama orientation anchor is therefore not a fully independent check of absolute declination. Agreement away from that anchor can remain informative; the dependency does not invalidate all measurements or all changes through the sequence.

The younger-period offset cannot justify subtracting 280 years from the older Touchet ash fits or all regional dates. That would assume an untested constant error across the entire record. Nor does a local age-model discrepancy demonstrate a fabricated historical chronology. The two proposed Touchet fits must be tested against the actual reference-series versions, their age uncertainties and any shared anchors.

Finally, S170's volcanic comparison curve is not the Fish Lake–Mono Lake curve fitted by S166. Importing its interpolated Table 2 values as though they were the original lake measurements would change the experiment. A useful reproduction requires the original lake directions, depths, dated materials, laboratory IDs, age–depth interpolation, orientation transformations and the exact series used in 2003. Exact 2003 inputs remain pending; the newly recovered archive version is insufficient for that reproduction. The magnetic-direction observations already recovered from Touchet are retained; this audit adds no new duration bound.

## A later, explicitly revised version

[Nilsson et al. (2010), S172](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2010GC003118), section 2.1, describes recalibration with Stuiver et al. (1998), magnetic alignment with the Saanich Inlet chronology, and a uniform 280-year shift toward younger ages. The alignment information is attributed to a 2008 personal communication. The authors explicitly warn that the shift introduces older-period errors near the independently constrained Mazama tephra. Section 2 defines BP as calendar years before 1950; it must not be confused with the uncalibrated radiocarbon axis in S166/S171. The supporting-information list describes model-output VGP paths, not a table of original Fish Lake assays. Only these text sections and the supplement descriptions were inspected; model calculations and figures were not reproduced.

This documents a later processing lineage, **not the identity of the series supplied for the 2003 study**. Agreement with Saanich after magnetic alignment would not independently validate that alignment. Preserve separate identifiers for the NOAA export, the unknown 2003 input and this later revised version. None can silently replace another.

[Walczak et al. (2017), S173](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/413173/1/Walczak_et_al._including_Xuan_2017_EPSL.pdf), manuscript PDF page 22, describes a further variant: the Hagstrum–Champion adjustment for the upper record and IntCal13 recalibration, addressing mixed radiocarbon and tephra ages. It also reports an approximately 5-degree inclination correction inherited from the original study, followed by interpolation for a regional stack. This page was visually inspected; the PDF has 56 pages and SHA256 `61118126f2936db623195b506e61c4824c29a96a9e554383bb3b6ede479f5924`. Figures and numerical processing were not reproduced. The text alone does not supply individual original assays, establish the oldest sample's age, or identify S166's input. Keep this 2017 version separate as well.

The next retrieval should therefore target **versioned sample data and age-model inputs**, not merely another curve labelled Fish Lake. Record the chronology's units, calibration, reservoir adjustment, magnetic alignment and orientation corrections before comparing it with S166. The existence of disclosed revisions is not evidence of concealed historical fabrication.
