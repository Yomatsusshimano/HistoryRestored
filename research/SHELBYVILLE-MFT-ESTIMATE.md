# Shelbyville: a proposed fill quantity, not a placement record

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). C030/R004; expanded inspection of S405. No independent review. [Selected forms and field ledger](../data/shelbyville-mft-estimate.json).

Four image-only pages immediately following the previously inspected April 10, 2019 email contain the estimate, engineering form and resolution it describes. They supply a proposed flowable-fill quantity. Their unsigned approval and certification fields do not establish adoption or execution.

## Retrieval and association

The [City Hall correspondence packet](https://edgarcountywatchdogs.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/To-City-Hall-part-2.pdf) contains 193 PDF pages. The covering email is PDF36. **PDF37–40 were rendered and visually inspected** in this follow-up; text extraction for these four pages was blank. The packet is a third-party reproduction, not an authenticated original municipal mailbox export.

The forms share section **19-00000-00-gm**, City of Shelbyville, Shelby County, and a January 1–December 31, 2019 maintenance period. Their types and adjacency agree with the email's description of an estimate, resolution and engineering agreement. This is a candidate attachment association. The original MIME attachment named `doc20190410072826.pdf` was not separately extracted or byte-matched; adjacency alone is not definitive file identity. These pages expand S405 rather than becoming new independent sources.

## What this changes

The record now supplies an explicit **planned** quantity and a narrower documentary search key, section 19-00000-00-gm. It does not assign 200 cubic yards to the 212/216 vault, a natural mud layer or any completed operation. The maintenance-year range cannot date a sediment event. No thickness is calculated by dividing this proposed program quantity by a chosen parcel area.

The estimate's sidewalk-repair inspection-required cell says **No**. That is a field on this estimate, not proof that nobody inspected the vaults or a reason to ignore the separately documented 2020 engineering inspection. The maintenance form's general services include preparation of expenditure documentation. Such final records, alongside approved/revised estimates, invoices and material/placement tickets, are the next targets.

No approval, actual amount placed, parcel allocation, historical construction date or catastrophic deposition mechanism has been established by these forms. All twenty objectives remain active.
