# Roopkund: numbered skull observations and review-access limits

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). C031/R006, S410–S411. No independent review of this archive. [Retrieval and selected specimen ledger](../data/roopkund-collection-controls.json).

## A collection trail with its own numbering

Alka Barthwal and S.R. Walimbe's [2023 chapter](https://www.heritageuniversityofkerala.com/Publications/P1/42_Barthwal%20and%20Walimbe_PPJ_20230131.pdf), printed pp805–820, is an original pathology report rather than a repost of the 2019 dating workbook. PDF1/4/5/11/12 were visually inspected; other pages were text navigation only. Selected observations are retained as source reports, not independently confirmed diagnoses.

The authors report 27 cranial samples collected from the surface during 2004–2007, with mixed fragments subsequently sorted and joined using adhesive. PDF11–12/Table4 supplies Roman specimen numbers: VII has a completely healed fracture; VIII and XII have remarks proposing injury as a possible death cause. These numbers do not establish a crosswalk to the 2019 R/I bone identifiers or its three unhealed cases. Collection years are not death years. Author-sharing between reports does not prove specimen identity.

This supplies a narrower target for collection-file retrieval. Recovery association and later laboratory reconstruction must remain distinct. The chapter's anatomical age estimates describe life stages, not calendar dates. A healed lesion cannot automatically become the lethal injury; the chapter's possible causal remarks are not confirmed causes. None of these selected entries supplies a dated mud deposit.

The earlier statement that no identifiers occur in the inspected 2019 trauma passage remains accurate. This new chapter adds identifiers within another numbering system; it does not resolve the missing join. The chapter's porosity diagnoses and environmental interpretation require their own specialist audit before adoption.

## What the public peer-review file actually contains

The [publisher-advertised 2019 review file](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-019-11357-9/MediaObjects/41467_2019_11357_MOESM2_ESM.pdf) has two pages, both rendered and visually inspected. It repeats a reviewer's favorable conclusion, gives another reviewer's entry as n/a, and includes a general author acknowledgement. Its editorial note says review previously occurred at a different journal whose process was not transparent. The earlier detailed questions and responses are not supplied here.

Consequently, this file cannot establish how any specific dating, reservoir, collection or injury objection was answered. Nor does the limited file prove that objections were absent or unanswered. Do not infer the earlier journal, its decision, reviewer identity or motive. The original study's peer-review status does not count as independent review of this archive's calculations, and objective14 remains incomplete.
