## Date and source lineage

An indexed [Chicago Historical Society encyclopedia description](https://www.encyclopedia.chicagohistory.org/pages/3292.html) identifies Edward Mendel and ICHi-00698, but describes1857. Direct page access timed out; this remains **search-excerpt metadata**, not an inspected accession record or institution-delivered image. S242's university page instead labels1860. S240 attributes lithograph completion/sale notices to May12/17,1860. The latter originals remain uninspected. Retain both date labels; do not assign1857 or1860 as a directly measured or printed date.

The shared firm names and320ft frontage strengthen the source-level association with the Lake Street operation. They do not independently authenticate its performance. The print may derive from the same publicity, observations or images used in the newspaper and book; the commission and image-transfer chain remain unverified. Multiple copies must not be counted as independent confirmation.
