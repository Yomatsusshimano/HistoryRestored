## Original definitions narrow the later citation

Printedp4uses1977/1987photographs, enlarged to1:4800. The shoreline proxy is approximate high tide inferred from wet sand or flotsam. The vegetation line is separately plotted. Printedp8states photo accuracy is at best approximately±50feet, warns that seasonal beach fluctuation exceeds50feet, and contrasts endpoint photographs with annual same-season beach surveys. This is the author's method/accuracy statement, not an independently measured error distribution.

Printedp11defines a western continuous-vegetation edge. The390ft(1977) and280ft(1987)vegetation-to-water distances are reported for **Long Beach**, alongside roughly20ftbeach accretion. The inspected original does not identify these as distances measured at Grassy Island. Later placement of the discussion in a Leadbetter habitat section cannot supply that missing location. Vegetation-edge movement and shoreline movement remain distinct.

The Fisheries table measures feet westward from fixed monuments to a beach elevation of+8feet, approximately high tide. Its caption does not specify a vertical datum or monument coordinates. This cannot be substituted automatically for NOAAmean-high-water or historical apparent-marsh lines.
