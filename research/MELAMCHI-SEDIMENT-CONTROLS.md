## Consequences for the physical model

Areal volume and local thickness must be tested separately. For an actual deposit, volume is the spatial integral of thickness over its mapped footprint; dividing by area gives an average only. A photograph cannot supply that integral without geometry and survey control. Flood marks require a separate observation series.

Different grain sizes do not by themselves rule out one flood: transport and deposition can sort sediment. Conversely, similar-looking sand at distant sites does not establish a common source or event. A proposed reconstruction needs geographically connected erosion sources, feasible routes, independently associated event ages and measured changes in thickness and grain size. None has yet been joined across the historical Reddit sites.

Next retrieval should prioritize the original elevation surfaces and deposit mapping, including acquisition dates, datum, resolution, registration error, vegetation/building effects and uncertainty propagation. The present report cannot yet calibrate a quantitative sediment-budget model. Its source photographs support a local comparison; they are not direct measurements or proof of an ancient global event.
