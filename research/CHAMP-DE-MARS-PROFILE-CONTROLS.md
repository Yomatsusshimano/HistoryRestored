# Original profile: section position, boreholes and unequal scales

Research draft, 2026-10-11. S357; C006. No independent review.

Two original plates from the bound Bulletin officiel de l'exposition universelle de1889 were acquired and visually inspected: [longitudinal profile, view104](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/sresrech.php?FOLXAE16.1/0104), and [grading/sewer plate, view142](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/sresrech.php?FOLXAE16.1/0142). A magnified detail of the profile's heading and central borehole labels was also inspected. Neither plate has an authenticated issue date in this audit. The catalog's1886–1887volume range does not date every investigation or insertion.

## Spatial controls supplied by the profile

Its heading places the section approximately50m upstream of the Champ-de-Mars axis and attributes the soundings to Eiffel's tower-foundation investigation. Selected borehole labels include20,22and24. This gives retrieval identifiers; it does not join them to Picard's50general pits or the newspaper's unspecified exhibition pits.

The printed length scale is0.0011per metre and height scale0.0055per metre. Their ratio is **5**: equal physical distances are drawn five times larger vertically. Apparent slopes in the diagram cannot be used directly as actual terrain slopes. Any quantitative extraction must account for scan distortion as well as the unequal scales.

The profile shows differentiated subsurface units and surface relief, with well/borehole annotations. A continuous drawn boundary is the compiler's correlation between observations, not proof that every intervening point was sampled. Coordinates, section endpoints, vertical datum, complete numerical logs and formation/deposition dates remain unjoined. This audit has not digitized the layer contacts or accepted all elevation labels.

## What the sewer plate does not supply

Despite its general grading/network title, view142 shows two dimensioned sewer cross-sections and a construction specification note. It is not a georeferenced route map or an as-built survey of the old conduits. Its reference to official tender clauses indicates a specification context; actual completion and the exact200mrelocation from S355 remain unverified.
