Research draft, 2026-10-11. S427/S439; no independent scientific review.

## Branch-specific engineering account

The [stormwater management plan](https://a2docs.org/file/223/Stormwater_Management_Plan.pdf), cover PDF1, is dated February1983 and prepared by McNamee, Porter and Seeley for the City of Ann Arbor. Its watershed description (PDF17/18, printed8/9) reports:

| Drain | Reported construction year | Named plan preparer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Allen's Creek | 1925 | Russell A. Dodge, Civil Engineer |
| Murray-Washington | 1927 | Menefee and Dodge, Engineers |
| Eberwhite | 1927 | Menefee and Dodge, Engineers |
| West Park-Miller | 1928 | Menefee and Dodge, Engineers |

These are **retrospective report statements**. Original drawings, contracts, dated inspection reports and completion certificates have not been inspected. The named engineers and years supply a concrete archival search target. The report distinguishes open courses, street-crossing culverts and later enclosure; this supports investigating construction in stages, without assuming that it explains every conflicting date.

The main drain is described as generally following the railroad from Stadium Boulevard to the Huron River downstream of Argo Dam. Pipe/culvert dimensions in the report describe engineered structures; they do not measure mud deposits. Branch names do not authenticate a particular building's foundation history.

The 1908–1916 map-based inference (S434), 1929 statement (S437), and modern1922–1926 collection attribution (S438) remain unresolved. Do not choose a chronology by counting repeated modern accounts. Identify the exact segment and distinguish street-crossing work, enclosure, extension, and completion before comparing dates.

PDF12/printed3 explicitly identifies previous1972,1974,1978engineering studies and a1981flood-insurance study as inputs. This source is part of an engineering lineage, not an independent observation of1920sconstruction. No hydraulic model has been independently reproduced from this source in the present archive.

## Factory creek-work account

Further inspection of the already registered [March21,1904 proceedings](https://aadl.org/sites/default/files/docfiles/Minutes_19040321.pdf), S427, PDF5/printed216, found an August street-work expense of **$13.55** for **“Labor on creek, A. A. Fluff Rug Factory”**. Nearby entries separately list creek cleaning at Huron Street and from Hill to Main. This establishes that the municipal account records creek work associated by name with the factory. It gives no parcel address, sediment description, volume, grade increment, work specification or independently checked execution.

The selected August sheet does not explicitly state its year. The following engineering report concerns the year ending January1,1904, but that is a separate report. **The factory entry's year remains null** pending its own heading/context. It must not be assigned to cleanup after the July1902flood.

The18-page delivery is discontinuous: PDF2 is printed210, PDF3 starts mid-list, and PDF4 is printed215. This limits access to the street report's opening context. The reason for missing intermediate printed pages has not been established. The file hash matches the priorS427retrieval; this is expanded inspection of one source, not a new independent witness.
