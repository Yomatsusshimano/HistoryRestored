# Lake Street newspaper lineage and unresolved differences

2026-10-09. C025 / S239–S240. This advances the retrieval chain; it does not authenticate an original newspaper issue or a construction contract.

## Access and comparison

S240 is a modern [transcription compilation](https://www.nike-of-samothrace.net/csc.html). Its selected March–May1860 entries were read through web access. Direct HTTPS acquisition failed certificate validation, and the linked April2 clipping was not visually inspected. An image successfully retrieved from another compilation proved to be a building illustration, not a newspaper clipping. No original-issue scan status is assigned.

The compilation attributes *The Great Building-Raising* to April2,1860, front page, column3. That is a retrieval target, not an independently authenticated date. Compare the [inspected1861 book excerpt](../sources/originals/chicago/barber-1861-lake-street-excerpt.pdf), printed1064–1065. The book identifies the newspaper but omits its issue date.

| Transcribed entry | Reported detail | Unresolved distinction |

| March9, front page column4 | Ely & Smith: east140ft; Pullman & Moore: west100ft; Brown & Hollingsworth:80ft; Carter & Bauer supervise; about$16,000 excluding masonry | Prospective estimate; original contracts absent |

| May12 and May17 | Mendel lithograph described as made, then offered for sale | Does not authenticate book photograph or establish identical image lineage |

These are related newspaper reports, not independent confirmations. The book reproduces much of the April2 narrative, but omits contractor/price passages and one four-story, three-store subdivision appearing in the transcription. Its13-store total and subdivision list therefore need reconciliation against the original issue. Omission in an abridged account alone supplies no evidence of deliberate historical fabrication.

## What to retrieve next

Retrieve March9, March26, March29 and April2 full pages with mastheads, page numbers and adjacent columns. Read original print before treating discrepancies as corrected measurements. Then seek separate contracts for each named firm, masonry work, grade elevations and payments; determine whether quoted costs cover lifting alone or broader improvements. The frontage allocation totals320ft, but arithmetic does not authenticate boundaries, firms or performance.

The Library of Congress [digitized-title listing](https://www.loc.gov/collections/chronicling-america/titles/?location_state=illinois&sb=title_s_asc&searchType=advanced&st=table), as returned by web search, lists the earlier title for January–March1859 and the later daily title from October25,1860. Direct catalog requests returned403. This indicates a likely gap in that particular digital route, not absence of surviving March–April1860 newspapers. A [University of Pennsylvania serial guide](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/serial?id=chicagotribune), successfully read separately, links the later title's1860–1864 run; its broad year label does not establish access to the target April issue.

No archive request, paid access, third-party message or physical investigation was made. Fill source, exact building lots, physical fabric and the original photograph remain unverified. The full twenty-part investigation remains active.
