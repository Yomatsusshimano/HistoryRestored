# Cascadia 1999 report: a processing lead that omits processing

2026-10-09. S232 is David Yamaguchi's contribution, printed pp.53-57, to the [1999 Paleoseismology Workshop proceedings](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/1999/0400/report.pdf), USGS Open-File Report99-400, edited by Kenji Satake and David Schwartz. Its title includes tree-ring data processing, but its opening paragraph explicitly says that topic was omitted and had not yet been reported in detail. This source therefore does not supply the missing original processing settings or indices.

Printed p.54 distinguishes Japanese records supplying the calendar date/time from tree-ring endpoints used to support the source association. It reports a resident's tip about a likely earthquake-killed, rooted redcedar near the beach at LaPush, about100km north of the then-current sampling limit. This is a proposed sampling extension, with no specimen ID, measurement series or final-ring result supplied. It cannot be added to the dated-tree count.

Printed p.55 describes a planned exploratory visit to Japanese sites/documents and comparison with the1960 Chilean tsunami. Planned inspection is not evidence that it occurred. The contribution is by an author of the1997 study and repeats that study's results; it is not independent replication.
