# Sacramento: construction records and material-source controls

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). Reddit lead R008; S453, S458-S460. No independent review.

The Reddit lead now has specific construction-record retrieval targets. They are not yet original ledger measurements, and the pictured demolition parcel remains unidentified.

## Keep the record series separate

[S458, Center for Sacramento History researcher resources](https://www.centerforsacramentohistory.org/content/dam/cityofsacramento/csh/forms/Researcher%20resources%202026%20new.pdf), PDF2 visually inspected, describes property-tax rolls and digitized map books. These can help identify owners and parcels; they do not automatically supply street-fill accounts. Listed permit coverage starts later than the 1860s works. The filename contains 2026; an exact publication date is unverified.

[S460, City Assessor finding aid CTY0005](https://oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark:/13030/c85q52wp/), collection description and arrangement read, lists special assessments beginning1868. That series cannot simply be equated with the earlier accounts underlying the [1867 newspaper quantities](SACRAMENTO-1867-FILL-QUANTITIES.md). Neither a catalog entry nor a broken holdings-guide link establishes absence of the original records.

## Materials and transport: claims that can be tested

[S453 district assessment](https://sacramento.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?clip_id=2745&meta_id=369761&view_id=30), newly inspected PDF17, attributes street-fill dirt to local farms or the American River bed and paving gravel to Folsom. It describes teamsters, scrapers, carts and wheelbarrows. Footnote71 points to Lagomarsino's *Early Attempts*, p58; note72 to pp100-101. That underlying thesis and original haul records are uninspected. **Folsom paving gravel must not be treated as the source of all street fill.** No measured extraction volume, travel distance, fleet capacity or compaction factor is established.

These references make the construction account testable. They do not independently verify logistics. Next inspect the cited roll pages and material-source references, identify the Reddit parcel, and compare original grades with logged strata before attempting a fill budget. Natural flood deposits and subsequent engineered fill can coexist.
