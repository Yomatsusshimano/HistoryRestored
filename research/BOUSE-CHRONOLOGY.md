## Original zircon study: limited access

S195, [Harvey 2014](https://tectonics.caltech.edu/publications/pdf/harvey_Geosphere_2014.pdf), DOI 10.1130/GES00904.1, abstract/introduction PDF p.1, reports comparing Bouse ash at Amboy and Buzzards Peak with candidate tephras using zircon U-Pb crystallization ages, U-Th abundances and oxygen isotopes. It supports correlation to Lawlor's 4.834 ±0.011 Ma argon eruption age; these are different dated quantities. The reported oxygen contrast rejects a Heise candidate. This is more than fossil-range dating alone.

Selected methods/results text returned in search retrieval describes selecting zircon with adhering glass and excluding eight older proximal grains before obtaining 4.94 ±0.04 Ma (44 grains; MSWD 1.41). That result is a lead for full-table verification, not a reproduced estimate. Do not substitute it for the eruption age. Local download failed certificate validation; subsequent web retrieval/screenshots failed. The first-page extracted text was accessible, but no complete local PDF, visual figures or supplemental analyses were inspected.

## Implications for the competing claims

An underlying dated rock gives an older limit on overlying deposition if the stratigraphic relationship is secure. By itself it supplies no younger limit: “younger than an ancient basalt” does not exclude historical deposition. Conversely, a primary ash horizon tied securely to an ancient eruption would challenge a historical age for that horizon. Transferring that constraint to another bed requires a demonstrated correlation and ordering.

The fossil-range argument needs taxonomic verification, sample context, the calibrated range and tests for reworking. An older fossil in a younger bed is possible; evidence must decide whether that happened here. It cannot be assumed solely to preserve a preferred date.

Zircon crystallization, eruption, ash deposition and later reworking are distinct events. A correlation based on multiple properties can strengthen source identification without directly dating every intervening process. Matching properties from the same crystals are also not wholly independent votes. The available evidence neither justifies collapsing these clocks into one number nor discarding them all because the sedimentary environment is disputed.

For H2-T in the [Bouse comparison](BOUSE-DISCRIMINATION.md), the next decisive work is sample-level verification of the ash correlation, primary-deposition evidence and stratigraphic transfer to the specific fossil beds. Even a secure local ancient marine incursion would not establish the historical map's northern Gulf-to-Pacific route, H2-G.

Next recover Harvey's full tables/supplement and Sarna-Wojcicki et al.'s argon analyses, including standards, corrections and uncertainty conventions. Compare candidate tephras and excluded grains explicitly; then inspect measured sections behind the competing ash placements. No confidence bound for the formation's entire duration or corrected global chronology is calculated here.
