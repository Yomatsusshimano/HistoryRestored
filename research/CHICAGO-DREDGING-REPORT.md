## Printed date conflict

PDF210 prints November1865 for the superintendent's report, followed by assessment confirmation in March1857. PDF212 prints November1856 for that report. Both readings were visually checked. November1856 fits the narrated order, but an original council entry or earlier edition must test that interpretation. No silent correction is made. This is an internal record discrepancy, not evidence establishing a global chronology transformation or intent to fabricate.

## Connection still required

The [1859 Haines address](CHICAGO-STREET-GRADE.md) reports North Branch claims and an October1857 assessment. Maher describes a March1857 confirmation. Related legal doctrine does not establish the same reach, contract or assessment. The sources can share municipal records and are not automatically independent witnesses.

No spoil destination, street-fill delivery, excavation geometry or current physical contact was recovered. Next obtain the superintendent's report and profiles, surveyor's measurements, May1857 contract,1862 vouchers, and disposal records. Match lots and river reach before connecting quantities to a named street. A dredged-volume-to-street-thickness calculation would currently require invented area, bulk-volume change and disposal fraction, so none is presented.
