# Pioneer Square: separating construction records from burial claims

2026-10-08. C001 remains a sourced draft without independent review.

The existing SDOT account attributes many areaways to engineered street raising. A below-sidewalk room alone cannot determine whether its surroundings were filled deliberately, deposited naturally, or changed in several stages. Parcel-specific elevations, fabric and dated work records are needed.

## Pre-fire administrative evidence

[Council minutes of June 4, 1883, p.273 (S85)](https://fromthepage.com/sma-archives/city-of-seattle-records/seacpm18830604/display/1281394?translation=false), inspected as a transcription, record requests concerning Washington and Main west of Commercial, a surveyor's grade-line report referred for an ordinance, and a recommendation to grade Jackson Street. The Columbia Street bidding entry lists G. N. Alexander & Co. offering earthwork at 38 cents per cubic yard under Ordinance 386. A bid price establishes neither excavated quantity nor payment or completion.

[Proceedings of August 24, 1883, p.342 (S86)](https://www.fromthepage.com/sma-archives/city-of-seattle-records/seacpm18830824/display/1281477?translation=false) record adoption of recommendations for 30-foot-wide waterfront wharves or roadways: 110 feet westward from Jackson Street's bulkhead and 100 feet westward from the alley in Maynard's Block 2 on Washington Street's south side. They also record adoption of an ordinance establishing a grade on part of Jackson Street. The page supplies no grade elevations, fill volume or completion certificate. A wharf or roadway is not necessarily a mass of earth fill.

Both sources document pre-1889 planning activity, subject to transcription accuracy. Neither dates the enclosure of a particular present-day areaway. The linked image buttons led to example-image 404s; handwriting was not visually checked. Do not treat these transcriptions as inspected scans or combine different street projects into one event.

## A route to parcel-level evidence

The [Areaway Records finding aid, series 0605-01 (S84)](https://archives.seattle.gov/finding-aids/repositories/2/resources/1919) identifies applications from 1905–1921, sometimes accompanied by drawings, and a second volume indexed by street. It describes entries containing permit/date, property and legal-description identifiers. These are catalog statements; no individual application or drawing was inspected.

The next discriminating check is to select one indexed parcel, retrieve its application and drawing, and match it to original grade specifications, completion records and surviving wall geometry. Compare intended and surveyed elevations using a known datum. Inspect fill descriptions for placement methods and stratigraphy. A permit date may concern maintenance of an older space, so it cannot automatically serve as its construction date.

The catalog references Seattle Ordinance 12022. The attempted Clerk page returned a human-verification challenge and supplied no ordinance text. Its date and requirements remain unverified here. Other jurisdictions' same-number ordinances are not substitutes. No outside contact was made.

## Grand Central Block: observed fabric versus proposed repairs

[Clark Barnes's 2020 board briefing (S87)](https://www.seattle.gov/documents/departments/neighborhoods/historicpreservation/historicdistricts/pioneersquare/meetingdocuments/grand_central_block_board_briefing.pdf), pages 26–27, was retrieved and visually inspected. Photographs show masonry ceilings, metal members, corrugated panels and patches. The authors describe replacement of brick arches at tree wells. The plan distinguishes Buttnick, City Loan and Grand Central conditions, giving a specific block for permit and alteration-record matching.

The adjacent drawings propose support, new footings and concrete over metal decking. These are design details, not completed-work records or sediment sections. Photographic material contrasts suggest alterations worth tracing, but cannot establish their dates. No original grade datum or surrounding fill sample is supplied by these inspected sheets.

[Structured observations](../data/grand-central-areaways.json) distinguish visible features, author interpretations and proposed work. Match individual compartments to dated permits and as-built alterations before using their appearance to infer burial history. The broader catastrophe claim remains untested at this location.
