## What the clipping reports

The article says the project's first phase began in 2020 and filled vaults beneath downtown sidewalks before streetscape work proceeded. It also asserts that the street had previously been a story lower and was covered with pavement. That historical statement has no cited original survey or date in the inspected portion and is not a direct attributed quotation.

These have different evidentiary roles: contemporary reporting of recent work, and a retrospective grade-history claim requiring its own records. Neither supplies a measured natural sediment horizon.

## Discriminating next records

Seek the complete original issue and its continuation, certified city approvals, revised/final section 19-00000-00-gm records, expenditure statements, work diaries and parcel-specific material tickets. For the earlier street level, seek dated grade ordinances, original profiles and survey ties to surviving fabric. Original image identity, a deposit profile, sample dating and a feasible common-event model remain separate requirements. A reported modern filling operation cannot itself explain the vault's earlier origin. All twenty objectives remain active.
