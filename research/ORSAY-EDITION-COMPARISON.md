# An attachment sentence omitted between dictionary editions

Research draft, 2026-10-11. S365 compared with S363; C006. No independent review.

The [1844 dictionary scan](https://archive.org/details/dictionnaireadm00lazagoog) was visually inspected on the original dated title and complete Orsay entry, printed pp.509–510: PDF pages10,498–499. Extra half-title, reverse and next-entry surfaces were inspected during locator correction. Other pages remain uninspected. [Comparison ledger](../data/orsay-edition-comparison.json).

The original p510 confirms a sentence attributing the island’s attachment to the site of the later second quay section to **1780**. It supplies no inspected execution account, material quantity or parcel plan; the physical extent meant by attachment remains unknown. The accompanying historical burial narrative is not used to identify Eiffel’s animal bones.

This is a bounded edition comparison, not an exhaustive textual collation. The length difference cannot be interpreted as land movement without checking endpoints and survey definitions. Omission does not identify an editor’s reason, invalidate the earlier statement, or demonstrate deliberate concealment. The island passage may occur elsewhere in1855; that has not been tested.

Keep1780attachment, the1859report’s1773wording, and Zunz’s1812completion claim as different reported propositions. They may describe different stages, but that reconciliation is unverified. The two editions share authorship and textual lineage; repeated decree extracts are not independent legal authentication. Original acts, works records, maps and exact foundation registration remain the discriminating evidence. All twenty objectives remain active.
