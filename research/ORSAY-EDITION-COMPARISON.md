# Island history redistributed between dictionary editions

Research draft, 2026-10-11. S365 compared with S363; C006. No independent review.

The [1844 dictionary scan](https://archive.org/details/dictionnaireadm00lazagoog) was visually inspected on the original dated title and complete Orsay entry, printed pp.509–510: PDF pages10,498–499. Additional original pages169–171/PDF174–176 were inspected to check the alphabetical boundary. The [1855 scan](https://archive.org/details/dictionnaireadmi00laza) was additionally inspected on printed304/PDF322. Extra half-title, reverse and next-entry surfaces were inspected during locator correction. Remaining pages are uninspected. [Comparison ledger](../data/orsay-edition-comparison.json).

The original p510 confirms a sentence attributing the island’s attachment to the site of the later second quay section to **1780**. It supplies no inspected execution account, material quantity or parcel plan; the physical extent meant by attachment remains unknown. The accompanying historical burial narrative is not used to identify Eiffel’s animal bones.

The follow-up check finds a separate **CYGNES (RUE DE L’ILE-DES-)** entry on1855p304. It preserves an island history, including the retrospective burial account, and reports filling at the beginning of the nineteenth century, followed by extension of rue de l’Université and quai d’Orsay over part of the former island site. That broad wording supplies no exact completion year. It does not repeat the exact1780attachment sentence. None of the burial narrative identifies Eiffel’s animal bones.

The1844alphabetical sequence onp170 proceeds from **CYGNE (RUE DU)** directly to the D entries; p169and171provide adjacent controls. No equivalent separate island-street entry occurs at this inspected boundary. This is not a whole-volume absence claim or proof that an editor physically moved the earlier paragraph. The retained1855island account narrows the earlier omission to the Orsay entry and rules out describing it as removal of the island history from the entire1855book.

This is a bounded edition comparison, not an exhaustive textual collation. The length difference cannot be interpreted as land movement without checking endpoints and survey definitions. The changed placement and date wording do not identify an editor’s reason, invalidate the earlier statement, or demonstrate deliberate concealment.

Keep1780attachment, the1859report’s1773wording, and Zunz’s1812completion claim as different reported propositions. They may describe different stages, but that reconciliation is unverified. The two editions share authorship and textual lineage; repeated decree extracts are not independent legal authentication. Original acts, works records, maps and exact foundation registration remain the discriminating evidence. All twenty objectives remain active.
