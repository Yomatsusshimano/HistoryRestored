# Archives, university and fine-arts projects in the printed 1812 decree

Research draft, 2026-10-11. S374; C006. No independent review.

Original page images of J. B. Duvergier's *Collection complète des lois, décrets, ordonnances, réglemens, avis du Conseil-d'État*, tome XVIII, **second edition, 1836**, were visually inspected: half-title PP7, full title PP9, and printed pages 126–129. The decree begins on p127 and ends on p128 before a separate cotton-warehouse decree. The adjacent pages establish item boundaries; the remaining volume was not inspected. [Original page 127](https://books.google.com/books?id=2aAxAAAAIAAJ&pg=PA127), [continuation](https://books.google.com/books?id=2aAxAAAAIAAJ&pg=PA128), [title](https://books.google.com/books?id=2aAxAAAAIAAJ&pg=PP9), and [retrieval ledger](../data/archives-duvergier-1812-program.json).

## What the complete printed item adds

The heading dates the item **21 March 1812** and describes buildings for the imperial Archives, university and fine-arts school. Its parenthetical reference is **Mon. no 87**. That is a retrieval lead to the Moniteur, not an inspected contemporary newspaper. No issue date has been inferred from its number.

| Printed division | Articles | Program and funding roles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title I: Archives impériales | 1–4, p127 | A left-bank quay building between Iéna and Concorde, intended to hold 100,000 cubic metres of Archives material. Construction in successive usable quarters; garden space reserved to permit expansion. Stone and iron, with no wood in construction. Plans due by 1 May; 200,000 francs previously allocated by the decree of the sixth of the month assigned to initial works. |
| Title II: Palais de l'Université impériale, etc. | 5–6, pp127–128 | On the same quay, coordinated with the Archives building: grand master’s palace, École normale, emeriti institution and University-related examination rooms. Project due by 1 May; available University funds assigned to it. |
| Title III: École des Beaux-Arts | 7–8, p128 | A coordinated fine-arts school on the same quay, with teaching rooms, exhibition gallery and workshops for painting/sculpture competitions. Plans due by 1 May; available credits from previous years’ budgets assigned to this school. |

This resolves the missing continuation of the four-article Archives quotation in [S365](ARCHIVES-PROGRAM-1844.md) and [S371](ARCHIVES-BORDIER-1855.md): this collection prints **eight articles across three titles**. It supports a coordinated group of proposed institutions, with distinct funding sources. The Archives allocation must not be applied to every neighboring project, nor confused with later Soubise expenditure.

These are requirements and allocations. They do not authenticate payments, a contractor's commencement date, completed quarters, a filled river channel, measured height or surviving fabric. The capacity figure is not a volume of excavated sediment. The site description remains broad; it does not establish surveyed parcel boundaries or overlap with an Eiffel foundation.

## Edition and wording controls

The inspected full title explicitly prints **Deuxième Édition**, **1836**, Paris, A. Guyot et Scribe. A later study used to route this search cites a second-edition tome XVIII as **1838**, p127. The delivered copy cannot be silently labeled 1838. Whether that reference identifies another printing or contains an error remains unknown; that later study is a navigation aid here, not a newly adopted source.

Article 2 in this copy reads **Les plans**, agreeing with the 1844 quotation and differing from Bordier's 1855 **Les palais**. This establishes an earlier printed occurrence of the variant. It does not establish copying direction, the authentic signed wording, the origin of the variant, or an intention to change history.

The title page says the collection was published from official editions and other legislative sources, but the particular item is attributed to the Moniteur. This is an original 1836 legal collection reproducing an 1812 measure, not the inspected signed decree. It prints no execution signatures or authenticated original archival identifier for this item.

## Separate records require separate authentication

The previously located CNAM Bibl. 145 catalog describes a same-date Saint-Martin lycée decree extract and a planned machinery transfer to the Louvre. Neither Saint-Martin nor that transfer appears in this eight-article item. A shared date alone cannot make them the same legal instrument. The underlying CNAM extract remains uninspected and is not adopted as a new source in this audit.

Next retrieve the cited Moniteur item and the signed administrative text; then obtain works accounts and surveyed plans. Those could test the relationships among ceremony, actual commencement, interruption and abandonment, and whether any palace remains correspond to later excavation reports. No catastrophe date, inherited structure, or historical-fabrication mechanism is established. All twenty original objectives remain active.
