# Allen Creek construction photographs: catalog control

Research draft, 2026-10-11. S438; no independent scientific review.

The [city's historic Allen Creek page](https://www.a2gov.org/systems-planning/water-resources/stormwater/historic-allen-creek/) links its [interactive map](https://www.arcgis.com/apps/instant/sidebar/index.html?appid=fd15b447829449ef8fe1ecdc107083d8) and attributes enclosure photographs to 1922–1926. This adds a third date account beside S434's map-based 1908–1916 inference and S437's reported 1929. No reconciliation is adopted. These accounts may describe different segments or stages, or contain errors; contemporary construction records are still needed.

The public map data identifies webmap `20969a03bf9e496789b60420c2f616d0` and the [photo feature service](https://services2.arcgis.com/cenCPuKxOZuq6BCE/arcgis/rest/services/HistoricAllenCreek_PhotoPoints/FeatureServer/1). Object 5 is cataloged as **601 W. Washington St**. Its selected attachments were downloaded, hashed in the source ledger, and visually inspected:

| Object / attachment | Catalog filename | Visible content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 / 42 | Drain_15 601 W Washington St..jpg | Workers, timber forms, excavation, machinery and adjacent houses. |
| 5 / 43 | Drain_16 601 W Washington St..jpg | Machinery, workers, excavated material and a suspended bucket near houses. |
| 5 / 49 | Drain_12 601 W Washington St..jpg | Forms, workers and machinery beside the same recognizable house frontage. |

These scenes provide a lead to deliberate construction and ground disturbance at the cataloged location. They do not measure a flood deposit, establish a natural layer's age, or establish the work's individual capture dates. The related scenes must not be counted as three independent confirmations. No visible date or original accession caption was recovered in the inspected images; the feature's Notes field is null.

**Keep properties separate:** cataloged 601 Washington is not the 415 Washington soil-log site, the West Huron rug factory, or the 213–215 Liberty Schlenker candidate. A nearby photograph cannot supply any of those properties with a soil profile or construction history.

The map's historic and 1984–1985 photo layers share a feature-service endpoint. Object 7 / attachment 22, cataloged 314 W. Liberty, is a modern photograph of an interpretive sign covering several periods and locations, including a factory expansion by 1940. Therefore, a layer title or collection date is insufficient to assign every attachment to 1922–1926. The sign is retrospective interpretation, not a contemporary construction record.

Next: locate original photo accessions and dated captions, then join a specific creek segment to contracts, engineering plans or as-built records. The date conflict and the earlier well-log correction hold remain open. Original Reddit photographs and their exact building match remain unresolved.
