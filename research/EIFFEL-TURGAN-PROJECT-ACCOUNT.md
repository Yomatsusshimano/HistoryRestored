# A tower proposal account and a bound-volume date problem

2026-10-11. C006 / S343–S344. Selected original scans and separate catalog metadata; no independent review.

Searching for the 1889 Pompey booklet located a digitized Eiffel article in [CNAM's volume 18 of *Les grandes usines*](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=4KY15.18). Its first page identifies livraison 353. Printed pages 25–28 describe a proposed 300-metre tower, show a design illustration, state a proposed price of **3,150,000 francs**, and name collaborators. This is an account of a project expected to be built, not a photograph or observation of a completed tower.

| p28 / vue 183 | Gobert, Nouguier, Koechlin and Salles named with roles | Published attribution, not payroll or authenticated participation records |

There is a chronology/version problem to resolve. The bound-volume title page, vue 4, visibly prints **1882**. A [separate ANMT catalog entry](https://pmb.culture.fr/opac_anmt/index.php?id=115316&lvl=bulletin_display) describes livraisons **353–354**, March 1886, 22 pages, with copy reference **2021 6 54**, barcode **gen34311**. The CNAM article ends at printed page 28. Neither a shared livraison number nor a catalog date authenticates identity of these versions. Binding history, reissues, added pages or catalog errors are possibilities to test, not explanations established here. Do not date the tower proposal to 1882 from the bound title, or silently assign the ANMT March 1886 date to every CNAM page.

The proposed 3,150,000 francs must remain separate from the [1900 supply table](EIFFEL-SUPPLY-RECONCILIATION.md), its conflicting adjacent prose, and the broader final project costs. Different dates and undefined accounting scopes prohibit treating their difference as missing money or direct cost overrun. A proposal is not a receipt.

The original Pompey target also became more specific: [ANMT bulletin 87255](https://pmb.culture.fr/opac_anmt/index.php?id=87255&lvl=bulletin_display) lists a Fould-Dupont Pompey issue of *Les grandes usines*, June 1889, with a separate publication-date field of 11 June 1889, copy **HP 889**, barcode **gen5777**. The exact day remains a catalog assertion. Its contents were not acquired. Its equivalence to [42 J 51](POMPEY-42J-ARCHIVAL-ROUTE.md), the 32-page 1889 booklet, remains unverified.

The CNAM volume listing ends with an 1892 Trayvou monograph rather than a complete run of the later monthly issues. That particular scan is not the Pompey booklet. A Calaméo search result titled *Les forges de Pompey* belongs to Ville de Nancy and its downloaded metadata describes a one-page 2015 publication; it was not accepted as the 1889 original. No original Pompey acceptance-test count has been verified.

Six relevant CNAM surfaces were visually inspected: title vue 4, article first page vue 148, and vues 180–183. Two other acquired surfaces, vues 3 and 147, are respectively a printer page and the end of the preceding Haviland article; neither supplies the Eiffel issue date. Full article and volume inspection is not claimed. [Acquisition and version ledger](../data/eiffel-turgan-project.json) preserves all eight acquired image URLs and hashes without mirroring them.

Next compare ANMT 2021 6 54 with CNAM's complete article, including wrappers, publisher notices and the transition around page 22; obtain HP 889/gen5777 and match its title, pagination and imprint to 42 J 51. Until then, this strengthens the documentary design/collaborator trail while preserving a genuine source-date discrepancy. It establishes no fabricated chronology, inherited tower, batch delivery, independent scientific acceptance or goal completion.

Follow-up [version controls](EIFFEL-TURGAN-VERSION-CONTROLS.md) establish continuous text after page22, later-year historical material elsewhere in the bound volume, and a cataloged28-page1886copy. These narrow the title-date inference while leaving the exact CNAM article date and ANMT version unresolved.
