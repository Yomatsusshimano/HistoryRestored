# Pompey supplier archive: retrieval targets and preservation limits

2026-10-11. C006 / S342. Finding-aid evidence; underlying records uninspected.

The [official 42 J finding aid](https://archives.meurthe-et-moselle.fr/sites/default/files/Contenu/Guide/042_J%20_Pompey_rep-methodique_v-publ.pdf) for the Aciéries de Pompey is a provisional methodological inventory dated December 2011. The 75-page PDF was acquired; title and printed pages 5, 6, 22 and 30 were visually inspected. Text extraction of the full PDF served navigation, not a claim that every entry was visually checked. This is the supplier-company fonds, distinct from the later Geindre research collection, 227 J.

For 42 J 276–279, the listed numerical sequences are 1–19,886; 19,887–34,941; 34,942–51,246; and 51,247–57,166. These are plan numbers, not dates. The group date range does not establish which volume contains 1886–1889 entries. Industrial plant plans are also not automatically tower-component drawings. Record references must be matched to actual dated sheets and subject descriptions.

Pages 5–6 describe uneven survival and custody. Transfers were often disordered and lacked transfer lists. The inventory records an April 1987 transfer of 63,500 microfiches covering the company's plan collection as updated through 1978. A separate September 1989 inventory allocated 5,340 retained documents among institutions after selection of physical plans. These counts describe different media and preservation operations; neither establishes survival of all historical originals.

The finding aid reports destruction of numerous archives before a November 1987 recovery of approximately 50 linear metres, mainly technical records from 1955–1978. Its warning about approximate descriptions and dates specifically concerns items 1148–1490, described using bundle labels. It should not be silently applied to every earlier item. Additional transfers and recovery followed in 1990 and 1995; the inventory remained provisional pending definitive arrangement.

This documents preservation limits, not a motive for destruction or proof of historical fabrication. Conversely, failure to find an Eiffel delivery entry in a provisional inventory cannot establish that none existed. A microfiche set may preserve information absent from surviving paper; matching the media, identifiers and custody history is necessary before interpreting absence.

Next inspect 42 J 51 and the relevant index pages of 42 J 275–279, preserving title pages, dates, plan identifiers, revisions and client references. Compare authenticated references with [Orsay supplier correspondence](EIFFEL-SUPPLIER-CORRESPONDENCE.md), factory acceptance and dispatch records. No tower order quantity, batch certificate, dispatch or newly manufactured component is established by this finding aid. No archival contact or physical inspection occurred.
