2026-10-09. C005/S331. Selected scans from Carol Seabury Prentice's 1989 Caltech dissertation inspected: PDF1,174,192-193,256 (printed165,183-184,247). Full261-page dissertation acquired locally; no complete review or independent scientific review.

The [institutional original](https://thesis.caltech.edu/238/13/Prentice_cs_1989.pdf) includes the laboratory report behind the repeatedly cited Ohlson Ranch fission-track age. Appendix B, printed247/PDF256, identifies sample **86-FR-1HP**, laboratory number **DF5762**, zircon, six grains, analyzed by C. W. Naeser. The report is dated May13,1988; the dissertation title page distinguishes 1989 from submission on December1,1988.

The laboratory row and dissertation's age discussion refer to the same reported result. Six grains are not six independent field samples. Grain-specific counts, calibration standards, irradiation geometry and complete reduction settings are not recovered here; no numerical age reconstruction or statistical comparison with later U-Pb/Ar-Ar dates is claimed.

## What the mineral date is used to constrain

Printed165/PDF174 describes zircons from one tuff bed. It cites preserved crystal faces and limited abrasion as grounds for probable original incorporation rather than detrital addition. Fine sediment, fossils and limited ash reworking in thin section support the author's quiet marine bay interpretation. These are source observations and interpretations; the specimens and thin sections have not been reexamined. A possible internal unconformity is explicitly uncertain because exposure and bedding are poor.

Printed183-184/PDF192-193 applies the ash age to a proposed shoreline-retreat and drainage history. Crucially, the ash predates those features by an unknown interval and is described as apparently near the section's top. The author therefore treats it as an approximate maximum age for retreat, not a directly measured retreat date. The reported12-20mm/yr slip estimate additionally depends on a proposed50km offset; neither the offset reconstruction nor its uncertainty is independently reproduced here.

## Comparison to the later Ohlson/Danville argument

The [2021 discussion already audited](DANVILLE-CORRELATION-REASONING.md) describes its Ohlson reference ash near the formation's base and reports zircon/biotite ages from a2012study. The1989near-top description creates a **bed-identity question**, not an authenticated contradiction. No retrieved record joins86-FR-1HP/DF5762 to OH-2A, the later dated zircon or biotite aliquots, or FLV119. Different beds, local sections or later reinterpretation remain possible; none is selected without evidence.

The dissertation narrows the retrieval task to a named sample and laboratory report. Next recover the field location and section position of86-FR-1HP, the2012dated-sample identifiers and analytical methods, and any explicit link to OH-2A. An ash age, chemical correlation and shoreline-retreat bound must remain separate until those joins are established. This source supplies no historical catastrophe date, continuous Gulf-to-Pacific passage or historical-fabrication mechanism.
