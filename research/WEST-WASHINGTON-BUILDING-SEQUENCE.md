## Keep the unresolved origins visible

PDF14 says the study could not establish who built the earlier concrete machine shop and privategarage; its suggestion of a previousowner is an interpretation. Missing earlierminutes do not establish deliberate erasure or ancient builders. PDF32 reports no coring/drilling and limited access to concealed/below-gradeparts. Therefore, neither foundation age nor a buried natural layer is established.

The report also has discrepancies: PDF14 starts its photo range inOctober1925 while PDF15labels anOctober1924image; PDF14identifies1926–1933minutes butPDF32mentionsrecordsfrom1924, perhaps a differentrecordtype; the panorama is datedJune1940 onPDF14 butmid1940s onPDF32. No resolution assumed.
