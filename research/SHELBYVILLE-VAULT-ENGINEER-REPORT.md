# Shelbyville vault: recovered engineer report and grade-history limits

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). C030/R004, S386. No independent review. Follow-up to the [identity and correspondence audit](SHELBYVILLE-STOREFRONT-IDENTITY.md). [Structured report controls](../data/shelbyville-vault-engineer-controls.json).

The February engineer report referred to in the earlier correspondence has now been located in a second public packet. It documents a constructed vault beneath a sidewalk, deterioration and a recommended filling sequence. It also supplies a historical claim about street-level buildup, which still needs original grade records. Its stated address is **212 E Main Street**, whereas the independently located Sit n Sip business address is **216 E Main Street**. Neither that discrepancy nor the match to the circulating Reddit photograph is resolved.

## Retrieval and document boundary

S386 is a [137-page public correspondence scan](https://edgarcountywatchdogs.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/To-City-Clerk-part-6.pdf). The complete delivered file was acquired with normal HTTPS and hashed. PDF9 and PDF11–17 were rendered and visually inspected. PDF10 was inspected as extracted text only. Other pages were searched for navigation; neighboring PDF18–19 were viewed only to locate the next document boundary, and their unrelated contents were not adopted.

PDF9's 13 February 2020 email, subject Sit N Sip, names an attachment **Shelbyville Vault Investigation.pdf**. PDF11–17 are a seven-page report, printed pp1–7, by **Civil Design, Inc.**, dated **12 February 2020**, signed **Tyler Ziegler, P.E., S.E.** The subject identifies 212 E Main Street. Its field inspection is dated **11 February 2020**. Printed p4 starts Attachment A; pp5–7 contain photographs. The next email starts PDF18. This sequence strongly associates the report with the email attachment, but is not an authenticated original email export or a separately acquired consultant file. The PDF exposes no embedded attachment names through the inspected PDF-reader API; the report is present as scanned pages, not an extracted embedded file.

## What was observed and what was asserted

Printed p1 describes a visual/hands-on structural examination, expressly without design information such as dimensions, member sizes or framing records. Its purpose was to assess condition and determine measures for safely filling the vault. Approximate reported plan dimensions are **28 feet east–west by 12 feet north–south**. These are horizontal dimensions, not burial depth or flood thickness. No measured vault height, original grade datum, sediment thickness, sample date or historic construction date is supplied in the inspected report.

The source describes three-course brick walls, brick arches on steel beams at approximately four-foot centers, and a roof supporting an approximately four-inch sidewalk. Printed p2's sketch locates seven arch bays, street/building walls and an access doorway. Its condition assessment distinguishes intact arches in bays 1, 2 and 7 from collapse or loss in bays 3–6, deterioration of the street wall, and sagging at the doorway. These are the consultant's contemporary observations and judgments, not independent measurements made by this investigation.

Printed pp5–7 show the vault's internal masonry/roof and doorway. Several photographs are dark or degraded in the delivered scan; captions are readable, but not every structural detail can be independently resolved. Printed p1 shows the facade and shades the approximate vault position under the sidewalk. The original Reddit image remains unacquired, so these report photographs are not declared an exact match.

**The historical assertion needs a different test.** Printed p1's Background says nearby property fronts originally had ground level at the basement base, followed by buildup to the basement top through individual vault construction. It gives no documentary citations, dates, surveyed old/new elevations or excavated stratigraphy for that account. The inspected constructed vault is consistent with a local engineered sidewalk support, but does not independently prove the entire grade-history narrative. The report's historical prose and its observed structural condition have different evidentiary strength.

## Recommendations are not completion records

Printed p3 recommends staged sidewalk removal, removal of timber/beams as necessary, temporary doorway bracing, a footer, blocking and sealing the doorway, wall repairs and flowable-fill placement. It recommends maximum **18-inch lifts** and treatment of voids below the roadway. Lift thickness is a construction instruction, not measured natural sediment thickness. The report warns that its list of potential concerns is not comprehensive.

Those recommendations provide a specific local reason for opening the sidewalk, removing structural fabric and later sealing an entrance. They do not prove which recommendations were executed, when filling happened, its actual quantity or the construction method/date of the original vault. Obtain the site diary, consultant correspondence, payment/acceptance records and dated photographs before claiming a completed operation. The separate June records for **222 Main Street** remain a different address.

## Revised next test

First reconcile the 212/216 identifiers using original parcel, business and building records and match the Reddit image to the facade. Then recover dated street-grade decisions, basement/vault plans and execution records. A natural-deposition account needs an in-place sediment profile and age association, not a conversion of vault width or repair lift thickness into a catastrophe measurement. A deliberate-grade-change account needs dated evidence too. This finding narrows the site's structural context while leaving original grade history and event association open.
