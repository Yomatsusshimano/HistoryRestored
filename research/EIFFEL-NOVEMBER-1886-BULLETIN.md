# A dated tower proposal: subsidy, estimates and source dependence

2026-10-11. C006 / S346. Selected original scans; no independent review.

The first issue of the [Bulletin officiel de l'exposition universelle de 1889](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=FOLXAE16.1) visibly dates its masthead **Saturday, 20 November 1886**, first year, number 1. Printed pages 11–13 contain a tower description, defenses of the proposal and an elevator discussion. The 1889 in the publication title names the exhibition; it does not date this issue.

The masthead contents explicitly places the tower article in the **nonofficial section**. Page 11 introduces it as a summary of newspaper reports with added information. This is a contemporary documentary witness, with declared dependencies and a promotional tone; the word “official” in the journal title does not make every paragraph a decree, committee minute or independently observed engineering result.

The reported subsidy is not the whole construction price. It must remain separate from the [3,150,000-franc proposal](EIFFEL-TURGAN-PROJECT-ACCOUNT.md), the [later iron supply account](EIFFEL-SUPPLY-RECONCILIATION.md), and broader final project expenses. Calling these amounts contradictory without matching payer, date and scope would manufacture an anomaly. The journal's claim that the expense would attract receipts does not prove those receipts occurred.

Similarly, the projected7-million-kilogram weight is not a delivery ledger. It cannot be subtracted from the later detailed weight total to establish missing or reused material without an authenticated component scope. Descriptions of absolute lift safety are advocacy, not an acceptance-test result. A public proposal for a helical lift does not prove that such a mechanism was installed in the finished tower.

This dates a public tower proposal and its financing discussion to the printed November1886issue. It is consistent with the [later progressive-assembly documents](EIFFEL-CONSTRUCTION-AUDIT.md), but it does not independently prove new manufacture. It also does not assign a publication date to the [CNAM Turgan article](EIFFEL-TURGAN-VERSION-CONTROLS.md): resemblance and shared forecasts may reflect common project or newspaper material.
