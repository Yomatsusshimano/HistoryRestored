# The 26 March 1813 letter requests plans, not a completion certificate

Research draft, 2026-10-11. S367; C006. No independent review.

The [scanned1868correspondence volume](https://books.google.com/books?id=xyRj3Fr1T4gC&pg=PA121) was visually inspected on its title PP9 and printed pages121–123. The title identifies volume25, published by order of NapoleonIII, with Henri Plon/J.Dumaine and an1868imprint. Letter **19759**, to **Montalivet, minister of the Interior**, begins on121 and ends on122; the following letter establishes its boundary. Page123 is an adjacent date/sequence control, not another witness to quay works. Public viewer images were acquired normally; no manuscript or full-volume PDF was acquired. [Acquisition and claim ledger](../data/cygnes-napoleon-1813.json).

The complete printed letter is dated **Paris, 26March1813**, matching the date of the [1899inventory’s quay-completion target](CYGNES-LAZARD-1899-INVENTORY.md). The shared date and related subject make it a candidate correspondence match. They do not establish that the Lazare collection holds this same text or document; that item remains uninspected.

## Funding and requested plans

| Printed statement, paraphrased | What it establishes at this evidence level | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A500,000-franc fund for the Ménagerie is suppressed and joined to the200,000-franc fund granted that year for quai des Invalides | Reported budget reallocation | Actual payment, expenditure or completed works |
| A240,000-franc fund is granted for quai des Champs-Élysées on the right bank | A distinct reported allocation and location | An extra island-filling payment or a combined cost for one operation |
| The Archives fund remains intact, allowing its foundations to begin that year | Prospective foundation work | Completed archive palace, precise start date or channel-filling completion |
| Montalivet is to bring a plan of the quay from Concorde to Iéna showing State/municipal property and land to buy for the Archives and regular buildings | Requested ownership and acquisition plan, with named endpoints | An inspected plan, authenticated parcel boundaries or their join to Eiffel’s later shafts |
| Plans of the Archives and the project for their emplacement are also requested | Additional design/site request | Executed buildings or an accepted construction account |

The letter refers to **quai des Invalides**, not expressly to quai d’Orsay, and does not name the island or certify completion of its channel filling. Its property-plan span is relevant to the river frontage under investigation, but it cannot be converted into an exact foundation-parcel association. Nor can the letter’s prospective archive-building foundations be assigned to any reported excavated masonry without location, depth and material controls.

## Printed provenance is a claim to trace

The source line beneath letter19759 says it was taken from a **minute in the Archives de l’Empire**. This is the edition’s provenance statement, not our inspection of an original signed letter or minute. No modern archival identifier is supplied in the inspected passage. The1868publication,1813letter date,1899inventory date and earlier physical works are separate chronological roles.

This result narrows the meaning one might otherwise attach to the inventory’s completion heading: a same-date published candidate concerns allocations and plans rather than certification. It does not invalidate a different uninspected letter, prove the channel remained open in1813, or establish that the1970account’s1812filling date is wrong. Channel filling and archive-building foundations are different operations.

Next inspect the Lazare item and trace the cited minute, obtain the requested ownership/site plans and actual works accounts, and register their parcels to the tower plan. The actual filling date, amount, deposit provenance and exact object joins remain unknown. No catastrophe or historical-rewriting mechanism follows; all twenty objectives remain active.
