# Lake Street raising: a named block in an1861 account

2026-10-09. C025 / S239. The earlier book lead is now a visually inspected source. [Selected source pages](../sources/originals/chicago/barber-1861-lake-street-excerpt.pdf) preserve the title and printed pp1063-1065 for public inspection.

## What was inspected

Barber and Howe's *Our Whole Country*, volume2, title dated1861, names New York publishers George F. Tuttle and Henry M'Cauley and Henry Howe in Cincinnati. This University of Toronto copy is distinct from the inaccessible LOC digital copy, not an additional independent account. Full archive PDF pages15 and301-303 were visually inspected. The file starts with out-of-order material, so printed and PDF pagination must remain explicit.

The engraving depicts a row of masonry buildings, inclined access ways and timber/work material at street level. It is a drawn/engraved transformation of a reported photograph, not the photograph itself. It cannot measure lift height, count hidden screws, identify sediment provenance or authenticate its own date.

The accompanying report is attributed to the Chicago *Press and Tribune*, under the title *The Great Building Raising*. The original newspaper issue has not been inspected; the book supplies no exact issue date here. The picture, caption and reproduced report are related records, not three independent confirmations. Assertions of no broken glass, uninterrupted business and estimated weight are retained as source claims, not engineering validation.

## What changes the test

This supplies contemporary documentary evidence of an executed lifting operation for a named block, extending the earlier proposed-work records. It challenges a claim that such changes could only have arisen through natural burial. It does not establish how every Chicago lower level formed, how this block was originally built, or whether earlier natural deposits occur beneath it.

The uneven-grade description on1063 concerns differences among sidewalks and successive grades. Its conditional citywide completion language cannot be treated as a finished, uniform fill horizon. Nor can the river-dredging quantities in [Maher](CHICAGO-DREDGING-REPORT.md) be assigned to this block without a disposal and delivery chain.

Next retrieve the original newspaper and photograph, block-level grade ordinance, contracts, foundation work and fill deliveries. Match a specific building/lot to before-and-after elevation and fabric records. Source-reported dimensions and labor counts alone do not establish load capacity, operating torque, transport budgets or an independently verified construction history.
