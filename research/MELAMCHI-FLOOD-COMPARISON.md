# Melamchi: a Reddit lead with a documented modern burial event

Research draft, 2026-10-11. R007; S443–S444. No independent archive review.

[The Reddit lead](https://www.reddit.com/r/CulturalLayer/comments/p6o5l3/) nominates buildings buried during a modern Nepal flood. Its linked media remain unmatched. A comment mentioning Colorado supplies no town, so no location was inferred.

[The original field study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11069-022-05433-5) reports the flood on **15 June 2021**. Section2.1 and Figure2's caption describe sediment entering buildings, fine-sand deposition reaching the first story, and a buried single-story building. Section3.1.1 reports69 surveyed reinforced-concrete buildings; inundation depths used tape measurements or story-count estimates. These are author-reported observations. The image itself was not successfully retrieved. Flood-mark height, riverbed elevation change and retained sediment thickness must remain separate measurements. A reported event date is not a laboratory deposit age.

This is a **positive comparison case for local flood burial**. It makes the physical possibility worth examining without assigning a cause to any older building. It neither dates the historical Reddit sites nor establishes a shared worldwide episode. Reinforced-concrete construction also limits transfer to older masonry buildings.

## What this changes in the investigation

The working comparison now includes deliberate excavation, constructed sidewalk vaults, heterogeneous fill, and a documented sediment-bearing flood. A lower window alone cannot discriminate among them. The next useful evidence is a joined record for the same building and layer:

- Identify the parcel and original photograph; retain capture date separately from upload date.
- Recover original floor and street levels, their survey datum, and alterations.
- Inspect profiles and sample locations; distinguish fill, in-place soils and water-laid deposits.
- Establish an event bracket from independent before/after records or properly associated samples.
- Test sediment source, transport route and spatial variation against specified alternatives.

These are retrieval criteria, not completed measurements or a prospective preregistration. They should be applied equally to claims of flooding and claims of ordinary redevelopment. Missing records cannot be counted as proof of either.
