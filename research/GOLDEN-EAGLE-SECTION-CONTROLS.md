# Golden Eagle section drawings: relative order and an omitted map

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York), S459 / Sacramento Reddit lead R008. No independent scientific review.

The [university report downloads](https://asc.sonoma.edu/historical-archaeology-golden-eagle-site) preserve useful excavation drawings, but both omit the oversized Trench E section. Selected drawings supplement the [deposit audit](GOLDEN-EAGLE-DEPOSIT-CONTROLS.md); they do not independently date its presumed flood deposits or authenticate the Reddit photograph's parcel.

Section16's datum is labeled **13 ft 0 in** at both ends. It is a local drawing reference; the temporary benchmark's tie to a recognized vertical datum remains unverified. No sea-level elevation, uplift, sediment volume or independently measured deposit date is calculated from it. Section16 directs the reader to Plan1 for section-point locations; that spatial crosswalk remains open.

The drawing and prose come from the same excavation report. Their correspondence is useful internal evidence, not independent replication. Relative superposition and cutting relationships distinguish successive activities, but do not turn the conditional1861-62 attribution of Layers70/143 into an absolute age. Mixed artifact bags described in the deposit audit remain a dating limitation.
