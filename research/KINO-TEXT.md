# Kino map volume: publication and Picolo's report

Research draft, 2026-10-08. C005 has no independent review.

## Inspected objects

The Rumsey collection groups the Kino map (11561.004) with a [volume title page, 11561.002](https://www.davidrumsey.com/rumsey/download.pl?image=/171/11561002.jp2) and a [composite of Picolo's report, 11561.003](https://www.davidrumsey.com/rumsey/download.pl?image=/171/11561003.jp2). Both JP2 images were retrieved. The title page and selected report pages were visually inspected at readable resolution; this is not a review of the entire volume.

The title page prints V. Recueil, Paris, Nicolas le Clerc, and M. DCCV (1705). This supports the catalog's volume date directly. It does not independently authenticate the binding history, establish the map's first publication, or date any coastline change.

The report's opening page, immediately before numbered page 249, attributes it to François Marie Picolo and labels it a translation from Spanish. Its heading and the closing on page 287 both give Guadalajara, 10 February 1702. This is a report date within a later printed volume, not an inconsistent second publication date. The underlying Spanish document and translation fidelity remain unverified.

## Short source passages and provisional translations

Transcribed from the scans; long s normalized. Translations are this investigation's and have not received independent linguistic review.

**Page 264, seasonal context:**

> Dans la saison des pluies, c'est un déluge d'eau; quand elle est passée, au lieu de pluies, la rosée se trouve si abondante tous les matins, qu'on croiroit qu'il eust plû

Provisional translation: During the rainy season there is a deluge of water; once it has passed, instead of rain the dew is so abundant every morning that one would think it had rained.

The sentence is seasonal testimony. Extracting the word “deluge” alone would erase its stated recurring context. No event date, deposit thickness, affected area or sediment measurement is supplied here. The testimony also does not establish measured historical rainfall or disprove all possible floods.

**Page 286, purpose of the report:**

> Je venois ici chercher des secours, sans lesquels il estoit impossible, ou de conserver ce que nous venions de faire; ou de pousser plus loin l'œuvre de Dieu.

Provisional translation: I came here seeking assistance, without which it was impossible either to preserve what we had just done or to advance God's work further.

Pages 265-267 praise pasture, water and agricultural fertility; page 267 also makes wider fertility conditional on cultivation and managing water. These are claims in a mission-support report, not a measured regional climate reconstruction. The report's purpose warrants comparison with independent observations, without assuming that its statements are false.

## What remains open

The inspected portions are the opening and page 249, pages 262-267, and pages 286-287. They do not supply a checked field itinerary establishing the northern land connection. That is a limit of this inspection, not a claim that the entire volume lacks such material. Recover the volume's other relevant sections, Kino's own dated observations, and the Spanish source before treating the map and report as independent geographic confirmations.

The catalog's unrelated 1884 atlas note remains a metadata conflict. The readable 1705 title page narrows that issue, but does not repair the catalog record or establish why the wrong note appears. Image identifiers and hashes are recorded in S38; images remain linked at their source.
