# Liberty Street: a 1904 sidewalk-grade control

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). S426–S430; follow-up to Reddit lead R005. No independent scientific review.

The [Ann Arbor council proceedings scan](https://aadl.org/sites/default/files/docfiles/Minutes_19040912.pdf), PDF1/2 (printed83/84), was visually inspected. The session heading dates the meeting September12,1904. PDF2 records adoption of a resolution to reimburse property owners on Liberty Street's south side damaged by sidewalk-grade elevation and to build cement protection around cellar windows at risk of water entry.

This supplies a documented local grading/protection mechanism to investigate. The resolution gives no addresses, East/West segment, height increment, exact grading date or completed wall record. “Schlenker” in the vote list is not a parcel identification. The South Ashley paving/excavation estimates on PDF1 concern a separate project and cannot measure Liberty fill.

The seven-page delivery is hashed in the [structured controls](../data/ann-arbor-schlenker-controls.json). A web-reader502 was followed by a successful normal public HTTPS download. The OCR/search excerpt's PDF2 header reads13; visual inspection reads12, consistent with the session heading. Meeting, original printing and digital indexing dates remain distinct.

Next retrieve City Engineer/Board of Public Works grade plans, affected-owner claims, disbursements and completion records; identify the segment through historical parcel/address maps. Only then compare original cellar sections, surviving fabric and measured elevations. This record does not establish the Schlenker opening cause, date a natural deposit, or authenticate the original Reddit photograph. It neither proves nor rules out a separate earlier flood. The full twenty-part objective remains active.

## Earlier paving and drainage records: separate project controls

The [March 21, 1904 proceedings](https://aadl.org/sites/default/files/docfiles/Minutes_19040321.pdf), S427, PDF1/6 (printed209/217), were visually inspected. The council ordered annual public-works reports printed in the minutes. The embedded Engineering Department report is dated January21,1904 and covers the year ending January1. It identifies Liberty paving **from Ashley Street to State Street**, with Lennane Bros. as contractor; its pavement summary reports **laid in1903**. The reported9,139.92square yards are pavement area, not sediment volume. This is a more precise project locator, but does not join that paving to S426's later sidewalk complaint or to Schlenker's parcel. Those joins remain unverified.

The [August 19, 1898 Ann Arbor Democrat](https://aadl.org/files/documents/pdf/aa_democrat/aa_democrat_18980819.pdf), S428, PDF1 masthead and PDF8/printed8 council column were visually inspected. Its unsigned “No Excitement” article reports adoption of a recommendation to extend the First Street culvert in front of Schlenker property. It separately describes tiling in a West Liberty alley between Main and Ashley. Do not combine those into one culvert location. The property has no first name, house number or boundary; identity with the surviving building is unverified. The article's “last Monday” wording is retained without assigning a verified meeting date. Adoption is not completed construction.

The complete18-page S427 and16-page S428 deliveries are hashed in the structured controls. Similar article text on S428 PDF16 was found by extraction only and is not counted as another witness; the complete packet's duplication has not been audited. PDF object-offset warnings did not prevent rendering the selected newspaper portions. S427 web-reader502 was followed by a normal successful public download.

These records refine local public-works search targets. They supply no measured sidewalk rise, natural depositional layer, original Schlenker opening date or authentic Reddit-image match. Next retrieve the corresponding original council/engineering records and historical parcel boundaries; compare projects only after their locations and construction phases agree.
