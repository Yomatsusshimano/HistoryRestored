# Liberty Street: a 1904 sidewalk-grade control

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). S426; follow-up to Reddit lead R005. No independent scientific review.

The [Ann Arbor council proceedings scan](https://aadl.org/sites/default/files/docfiles/Minutes_19040912.pdf), PDF1/2 (printed83/84), was visually inspected. The session heading dates the meeting September12,1904. PDF2 records adoption of a resolution to reimburse property owners on Liberty Street's south side damaged by sidewalk-grade elevation and to build cement protection around cellar windows at risk of water entry.

This supplies a documented local grading/protection mechanism to investigate. The resolution gives no addresses, East/West segment, height increment, exact grading date or completed wall record. “Schlenker” in the vote list is not a parcel identification. The South Ashley paving/excavation estimates on PDF1 concern a separate project and cannot measure Liberty fill.

The seven-page delivery is hashed in the [structured controls](../data/ann-arbor-schlenker-controls.json). A web-reader502 was followed by a successful normal public HTTPS download. The OCR/search excerpt's PDF2 header reads13; visual inspection reads12, consistent with the session heading. Meeting, original printing and digital indexing dates remain distinct.

Next retrieve City Engineer/Board of Public Works grade plans, affected-owner claims, disbursements and completion records; identify the segment through historical parcel/address maps. Only then compare original cellar sections, surviving fabric and measured elevations. This record does not establish the Schlenker opening cause, date a natural deposit, or authenticate the original Reddit photograph. It neither proves nor rules out a separate earlier flood. The full twenty-part objective remains active.
