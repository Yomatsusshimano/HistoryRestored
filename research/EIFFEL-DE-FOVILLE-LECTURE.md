## What kind of evidence is this?

On p345, de Foville says Eiffel facilitated site access and lent a model. On p352, he reports climbing three days earlier using the stairs while vehicles were still unavailable. This is a published personal-visit claim, not an authenticated diary or independent manufacturing inspection. Its critical comments on location and decorative arches coexist with advocacy for the tower.

Pages347–349 describe foundations, compressed-air excavation, workshop preparation and hydraulic adjustment. These give specific processes to compare against drawings and work logs; rhetorical confidence does not validate their engineering performance. The descriptions do not identify inherited fabrication or a catastrophic deposit.
