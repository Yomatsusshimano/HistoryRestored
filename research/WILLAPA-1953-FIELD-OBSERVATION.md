# A dated Grassy Island mean-high-water connection

2026-10-09 (America/New_York). Sourced draft; no independent review.

The joint T9634/T9637 field-inspection report explicitly records that the mean-high-water line had no break between Grassy Island and Leadbetter Point on June6,1953. It also says that a channel exists there at other times. This supplies a dated reported boundary configuration, strengthening the earlier intermediate-map finding while directly limiting a permanent-attachment interpretation.

The original [T9637 report](../sources/originals/cascadia/T-9637.PDF), S266, was delivered in NOAA's [T09637N package](https://nsde.ngs.noaa.gov/downloads/T-09637N.zip). PDF11, printed10, item7(a), specifies planetable location on a1:10000enlargement of photograph1613. The [acquisition ledger](../data/willapa-t9637-acquisition.json) preserves the archive and report hashes. PDF1-24of35 were visually inspected; PDF25-35remain uninspected. All-page text extraction yielded only two characters per page; no substantive fulltext or OCR review is claimed. The T9637N raster stops south of the target, but its bound report includes joint fieldwork covering the northern tip in T9634.

## What this resolves and what it does not

The [T9634 audit](WILLAPA-T9634-INTERMEDIATE.md) could identify a continuous sand outline and mixed source dates but lacked an explicit dated statement about the island connection. The recovered field narrative now provides that statement. It agrees with the June6date in S265's shoreline compilation, while the June30date on S265's topographic form still conflicts. This is not enough to identify the cause of the discrepancy or silently correct the form. Multiple records from the same fieldwork are dependent evidence.

The documentary conclusion is specific: a field report describes a continuous MHW boundary on June6,1953. It does not establish when a connecting bar first formed, whether it remained connected, its elevation at every tide, or when any intervening channel opened. It does not transfer a1939station date or a1950photo date to the physical configuration. Original photograph1613, the annotated enlargement and planetable sheet remain unrecovered.
