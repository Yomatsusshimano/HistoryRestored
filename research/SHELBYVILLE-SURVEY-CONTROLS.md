# Shelbyville: state survey records and neighbouring facades

Research draft, 2026-10-11 (America/New_York). C030/R004; S395–S397. No independent review. [Queries, selected records and inspection ledger](../data/shelbyville-survey-controls.json).

The state survey database now supplies specific nearby records and original linked survey material. The retrieved Lantz Brothers form identifies **222 E Main**, reports construction in **1893**, and distinguishes that from a business history beginning in **1855**. Those are reported dates for a neighbouring building and business, not a construction date for the 212 E Main vault.

## Public database query and its boundary

The state's [HARGIS page](https://dnrhistoric.illinois.gov/preserve/hargis.html) links a public map application. Its advertised web-map configuration identifies a [Surveyed Properties layer](https://services.arcgis.com/b9DHj1BjfdLLFv11/arcgis/rest/services/Properties_EDIT_v13_View-Surveyed/FeatureServer/0). The actual layer schema and its PDF/Photo relationships were inspected before queries.

A read-only query for City Shelbyville and StreetName containing Main returned **37 records**. No transfer-limit flag was returned; this is the delivered query result, not a census of all archival material. None of those records has StreetNumber 212 or 216. A second exact-number query for 212, 216 or 220 returned only **220 W Main**, which must not be mistaken for E Main.

These selected records label their survey date **1971–1975**. That range neither proves an individual inspection date nor dates every linked photograph. The layer has no attachments but has related PDF and Photo tables; an attachment-only check would have missed the accessible material. Original survey cards for 212/216 remain unacquired. Database absence does not establish absence of a building, original record or historical importance.

## Lantz Brothers original form

The advertised [109381.pdf scan](https://idnr.maps.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/6895eee79ce849ab8230606703f270be/data) has three pages, all rendered and visually inspected. Page 1 identifies the Lantz Brothers Building at 222 E Main and inventory notation SH-H-35. Page 2 gives the reported 1893 building date and 1855 business origin, and names **Beulah Knecht** as preparer. The preparation-date field is blank. Its account mentions three generations and several commercial functions, but the right edge of part of the statement is clipped; no missing wording is reconstructed.

No claim-specific original construction document is cited in the inspected form. The bibliography text below is explicitly a generic example for completing the form, not a cited source for the building. The third page is a heavily degraded photograph reproduction; the separately linked JPEG provides clearer facade context. The form's original-site/unaltered checkboxes are survey assertions, not proof that all present fabric is unchanged.

The reported 1893 date is consistent with the previously inspected Sanborn edition's **being built** annotation west of the block's internal alley. This is a chronological lead, not completed parcel registration: the exact historical footprint and surviving structure still require a deed/plan join. Business origin in 1855 cannot be substituted for building construction in 1893, nor can either date be assigned to the neighbouring vault.

## Linked photographs and limits

Three delivered JPEGs were visually inspected: the record for 218 E Main and two images linked to 222 E Main. Their complete byte hashes and dimensions are retained in the ledger. The 218-linked image shows a light-coloured ornate facade with two upper windows and neighbouring portions. Both 222-linked images show the broad Lantz facade with three central arched upper windows; one has a Sears sign. The images provide useful architectural landmarks.

The connection between each image and address comes from its related-table record. That is catalog attribution, not a certified survey. No inspected caption establishes capture dates. The survey-range field and modern upload dates must not be silently used as photo dates. The engineer report's 2020 facade photo was rechecked; no exact vault/facade match is adopted from this batch. None of these survey images exposes the vault or records a sediment profile or vertical grade datum.
