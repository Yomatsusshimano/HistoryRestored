# The 1855 quay account does not certify channel completion

Research draft, 2026-10-11. S363–S364; C006. No independent review.

The [1855 Lazare dictionary](https://archive.org/details/dictionnaireadmi00laza), second edition, was checked on its original title page and complete Orsay entry, printed pp.607–608. These are PDF pages11,625–626, not interchangeable locators. Other pages remain uninspected. [Acquisition and claim ledger](../data/orsay-lazare-1855.json).

The entry quotes successive quay orders. Its 11March1808 extract calls for construction between the Concorde and École-Militaire bridges, with completion directed within six years. Its 10February1812 extract orders a planted promenade between Concorde and Iéna, specifying a combined width of55metres. Neither inspected extract certifies completed channel filling. Authorization, a target duration and a promenade specification are distinct from execution and acceptance.

This is an original pre-tower publication reproducing earlier instruments, not the original decrees or attached plans. It does not independently authenticate the [1970 study’s reported1812completion](CYGNES-PARTIAL-FILL-ARCHIVAL-LEADS.md). The1844edition has now been checked separately: its original1780attachment sentence is absent from this1855entry. See the [bounded edition comparison](ORSAY-EDITION-COMPARISON.md); attachment extent and completion remain unverified.

## The archive reference has a source lineage

The [Archives de Paris finding aid](https://archives.paris.fr/_depot_ad75/_depot_arko/ead/INV0870.pdf), version13August2021, identifies D1Z as the Lazare collection, including originals and copies relating to the dictionary. D1Z88 covers Opéra passage–Ottoz villa. Only web-extracted title/presentation and the relevant inventory entry were accessed: native download returned404 and screenshots failed. The underlying volume and exact1773item remain uninspected. The catalog does not identify whether that item is an original or a copy.

The cited volume and dictionary may therefore share a documentary lineage; do not count them as independent confirmations without tracing the item. Retrieve the decrees, attached plans and works accounts, and inspect D1Z88’s relevant documents. Actual filling completion, sediment dates and foundation-parcel association remain unverified.
