# Can one upheaval explain the wildlife cases?

2026-10-08. Retrospective synthesis of C003, C009 and C011–C016. All remain sourced drafts without independent scientific review. This comparison uses the existing audited records; it adds no field measurements or new source discoveries.

## Specify which event is meant

H3 in [CLAIMS](../CLAIMS.md) needs three separate versions:

| Version | Concrete claim | Current assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H3-M: common mortality | The nominated animals died within one declared short episode | The selected sloth calendar sets cannot all fit an instantaneous, one-year, decadal or century-long window while remaining inside their reported sets. This is a descriptive incompatibility, not a probability of rejection. |
| H3-D: common later deposition | Remains of different ages were transported or buried together later | Different biological ages allow this logically, but no common horizon connecting all nominated sites has been established. It needs its own depositional dates, provenance and transport evidence. |
| H3-E: common population disruption | One event caused extinction or range change across the nominated populations | Individual deaths and last recovered fossils do not measure extinction. Sampling, survival probabilities, dated environmental change and causal alternatives remain insufficiently tested. |

Changing from H3-M to H3-D is a change in the tested explanation. Record it explicitly; do not call it a successful mortality prediction. None of these versions alone establishes historical fabrication or recovered technology.

The age labels span different objects, methods and uncertainty conventions. They must not be pooled into one numerical intersection or displayed as equivalent death dates. Missing age assignments, such as the hyena's direct date, remain missing rather than being filled from the preferred interpretation.

## Two existing calculations, different implications

The [sloth calculation](../analysis/sloth-intervals-result.json) finds a minimum window touching all seven published calendar sets of 5,910 years. Restricting to the six *Neocnus comes* rows still requires 3,870 years. Thus a one-year or hundred-year window cannot accommodate these selected sets without leaving at least one of them. The [duration check](../analysis/wildlife_duration_check.py) evaluates declared 0-, 1-, 10- and 100-year scenarios against those minima. They are retrospective sensitivity scenarios, not preregistered predictions or user-selected event dates.

This does not make the minima statistical lower confidence bounds: the published sets omit tails and share laboratory/calibration dependencies. It also does not show when the bones entered their final deposits.

The [Coyote calculation](../analysis/coyote-intervals-result.json) finds an overlap of 18.32–19.05 ka among five flood-sediment ranges. That result leaves common-age compatibility open within those ranges. It cannot choose one flood over repeated floods; stratigraphic separation must resolve that question. The interval overlap has no joint confidence level assigned here.

These findings support different assessments of different propositions. Counting both as generic evidence for a catastrophe would erase the actual tests.

## Decision and next discriminators

Do not use this selected inventory as a demonstrated simultaneous death assemblage. Retain common later deposition as unestablished, with explicit required evidence rather than an automatic explanation for every mismatch. Do not infer extinction or a worldwide geographic transformation from either outcome.

The highest-value next mortality check is specimen-specific preparation and later-redating review of the sloth endpoint samples AA-58439, AA-58434 and AA-58431. For deposition, audit Coyote Canyon field contacts, original mammoth assays and camel pooling/quality controls. For older geography, verify Thistle Creek ash chemistry and the camel exposure history. Recover specimen-level muskox metadata before drawing migration corridors.

This purposive collection is not a prevalence sample, a complete wildlife range map, a reconstructed lost geography or an independently reviewed synthesis. No held-out prediction has succeeded, and the original twenty objectives remain intact and incomplete.

## Moxee mortality versus deposition check

The [Moxee audit](MOXEE-MAMMOTH.md) adds physical weathering evidence and a reported collagen assay. It challenges treating fossil burial as automatically simultaneous with death. It also shows why a reworked tusk in an upper unit cannot by itself date lower units relative to the animal's death. Neither a delay duration nor shared regional burial is established.
