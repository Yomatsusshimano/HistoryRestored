# An Eiffel-supplied notice and an editorial repayment inference

Research draft, 2026-10-11. S352/S353; C006. No independent review.

The complete two-page [1890 operating notice](https://www.numdam.org/article/JSFS_1890__31__374_0.pdf), printed374–375, was rendered and visually inspected together with its digital wrapper. It attributes a quoted notice to Eiffel. The quotation closes after the operating-staff sentence; the following profit and repayment paragraph is outside that quotation. The article is signed **T. L.**; the initials are not expanded without evidence.

Its three visitor categories sum to1,953,122. Receipts6,509,900 minus expenses1,036,857 reproduce the editorial profit5,473,043. Calling this473,043more than cost implicitly uses a5,000,000-franc baseline, not a demonstrated total construction cost. S351 and S17 report different figures whose categories and periods remain unjoined. Neither this subtraction nor the editorial repayment assertion authenticates cash transfers.

The original reads **26May** for the East-pier lift opening; PDF extraction reads20May. The ledger retains26as the visually checked source reading. A published notice remains distinct from a commissioning record, safety inspection or independent confirmation of operation.

The same notice explicitly identifies its earlier lecture summary as January1889. [S353, printed30, section5](https://www.numdam.org/item/JSFS_1889__30__28_0.pdf), praises de Foville and presents prospective construction/visitor claims. That section and its digital wrapper were visually inspected. It is a summary, not the full lecture. It supplies no underlying invoice or capital ledger. The different Journal des économistes citation in S351 remains unresolved; the January1889reference must not silently replace it.
