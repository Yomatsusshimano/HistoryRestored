Research draft,2026-10-11. S436; no independent scientific review.

PDF245 cover, PDF267Figure3/A-3 and PDF290–292 were visually inspected. Installation date on all three logs is March27,1992; the location drawing is datedApril16. Surface paving/stones/concrete precede the selected intervals. These are transcribed log descriptions and contacts, not new measurements. The [structured dataset](../data/west-washington-1992-soil-logs.json) retains well IDs, locators and reported sampling intervals. Neither a printed depth axis nor an unbounded lower description establishes total bore depth. Surface and casing elevations areN/A; do not align layers across wells as common elevations.

Figure3 maps the wells around a former tank excavation at415WestWashington. It does not place them at the WestHuron rug factory or WestLiberty Schlenker building. Relative mapped positions are recorded; georeferenced coordinates remain unknown.

Cinders and wood in layers classified by the investigator as fill make disturbed-ground history a necessary competing explanation. Peat beneath fill in two wells offers a potential dating target if archived material with reliable provenance exists. The third log has no peat interval recorded, a difference to retain rather than smoothing it into a uniform layer. These observations do not independently date fill, establish1902flood deposition or rule out natural strata. No current contamination/safety assessment or sample availability claim is made.
