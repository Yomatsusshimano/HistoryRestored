# Subscriber-list excerpt and exact financial dossier

Research draft, 2026-10-11. Sources S348-S349; case C006. No independent review.

The financial archive displays a small original subscriber-list excerpt and identifies a company dossier. Neither establishes the total capital paid, construction expenditure or fulfillment of the State contribution. The exact dossier is a useful next target for testing those questions.

## What was inspected

[SAEF's document feature](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/saef/document-du-mois/la-dame-de-fer) labels its illustration B-0066514. Its 550 x 320-pixel image was acquired through the ordinary browser after direct HTTPS downloads returned a browser check. The image visibly bears the headings **Societe anonyme de la Tour-Eiffel** and **Premiers Souscripteurs**, with numbered rows, names/addresses and two monetary columns. Only the upper portion is shown; the fourth row is cut at the lower edge. No full-sheet date, total or complete list was inspected. Names and amounts are not transcribed from this limited-resolution excerpt. See [image locator and hash](../data/eiffel-saef-financing.json).

The [official emissions inventory](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/saef/compagnie-des-agents-change-service-des-emissions-et-linformation-financiere) identifies **B-0066514/1**, including **Societe de la Tour Eiffel (2277), 1889-1969**. The same reference includes another company's records. The holding identifier and company number should both be retained when locating the tower material. This is folder coverage, not the date of the illustrated sheet. The inventory notice is dated November 2012 and credited to Agnes D'Angio Barros.

The inventory describes this service's records as issuer-submitted documentation for listing decisions. Its general description includes legal/company records and financial information. That helps identify what to seek; it does not guarantee that a particular balance sheet, receipt or subscription certificate survives in dossier 2277. These are linked descriptions from the same archive, not two independent validations of financing.

## A specific difference between summary and agreement

The feature describes ownership returning to Paris in 1910. [The inspected 1887 agreement](EIFFEL-CONVENTION-1887.md), article 11, instead separates City ownership after the exhibition from Eiffel's operating enjoyment for twenty years from 1 January 1890. Those are distinct legal interests. The modern wording conflates them relative to that clause. Later amendments and actual handover remain uninspected, so this comparison does not establish the full subsequent legal history.

This is a traceable summary-versus-source discrepancy, not evidence of deliberate historical fabrication. The feature's claims about majority shareholding and first-year profit remain secondary assertions here; no original capitalization or operating account was acquired. Its publication date is unknown, and its anniversary wording is not used to invent one.
