# Thistle Creek horse: what dates the fossil?

2026-10-08. C016 / S33. Sourced draft, without independent review.

[Orlando et al. (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12323), pp. 74–75, reports a Yukon metapodial assigned to approximately 560–780 kyr BP through geological context. The associated Gold Run ash is reported as 735 ± 88 kyr BP. Figure 1b places the bone in Organic 1, above the ash, with a separate younger organic unit higher in the section. The authors also cite nonfinite radiocarbon results. These are different constraints, not interchangeable measurements of death.

The paper uses the fossil's assigned age to constrain evolutionary divergence estimates. Those estimates therefore cannot independently validate the geological age used as their calibration.

The [article mirror](https://gwern.net/doc/genetics/selection/artificial/2013-orlando.pdf) supplied the inspected text and visually checked Figure 1. The 2008 ash-age summary is now checked below; radiocarbon assays, raw dating data and the horse supplement remain uninspected. Exact horse coordinates and the uncertainty convention for the 2013 ash value remain unknown here; no precision was inferred from the small map.

For H3, the reported context challenges assigning this horse and Coyote Canyon fossils to one death/burial episode. This is a conditional comparison, not a newly calibrated joint chronology. Reworking or erroneous association would require positive site evidence. Neither preserved DNA nor a nonfinite radiocarbon result establishes an exact age by itself. Next: audit the original stratigraphic correlations, sample identifiers and assay limits before constructing a regional horse distribution map.

[Transcribed age summaries](../data/gold-run-dates.json) retain corrected and uncorrected UT1791 results separately. The uncorrected value is not a third independent sample. Using the two corrected results, an inverse-variance check gives 0.73638 ± 0.05525 Ma, rounding to the reported 0.74 ± 0.06 Ma. The table specifies one-sigma standard errors. The [calculation](../analysis/gold_run_mean.py) assumes zero covariance and checks rounded summaries only; shared calibration uncertainty requires further analysis.

The later horse article gives 735 ± 88 kyr BP. Its uncertainty is not explained by merely converting the 2008 combined result into kyr. Both values are retained; neither is silently corrected. Resolving this requires the 2009 Gold Run study and 2013 supplement. The accessible 2009 abstract and figure captions are leads, not a reviewed dating table; its download link failed. Regional ash correlation and local fossil association remain separate links in the evidence chain.
