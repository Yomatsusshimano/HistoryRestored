# First-year operating receipts: reconcile scope before inferring profit

Research draft, 2026-10-11. Expanded source S17; case C006. No independent review.

The 1900 account explicitly explains why its **6,523,847.40-franc operating-receipt total** differs from a **6,687,893.40-franc board-report total**: the latter includes 100,000 francs of cash paid on shares and 64,046 francs remitted to the exhibition and classified as expense. The difference is reproduced without treating equity subscriptions as visitor revenue.

## Original-page scope

Printed pp228-231, canvases 8976175-8976178, were acquired through normal HTTPS and visually inspected. They continue the chapter opened on the [previously inspected formation pages](EIFFEL-COMPANY-CAPITAL.md). Links and hashes are in the [transcription ledger](../data/eiffel-operating-receipts.json); the [calculator](../analysis/eiffel_operating_receipts_audit.py) reproduces the limited checks below.

The account attributes its receipts to Couder's report presented to the board on printed p227. That original report, underlying tickets, cash journals and bank statements remain unacquired. Reproducing the later narrative's arithmetic is not independent confirmation that money changed hands. This audit transcribes receipt summary and selected expense totals; it does not reproduce every weekly, monthly, personnel or expense row.

The souvenir entry is a margin: 63,572.15 minus 42,130.95 equals 21,441.20 francs. The summary is therefore not a uniform gross-sales measure. Ticket receipts also subtract the exhibition's share. Restaurant/concession entries describe payments to the company rather than those businesses' entire sales.

Ticket gross 5,983,930 minus 64,046 reproduces 5,919,884 francs. Adding 100,000 of cash share payments and the 64,046 exhibition share to the receipt summary reproduces the stated board-report total of 6,687,893.40. The 100,000-franc share-payment report agrees with the [capital allocation comparison](EIFFEL-COMPANY-CAPITAL.md), but those accounts may share company disclosures.

## Expense check and repayment limits

Printed p231 gives expense subtotal 452,529.01 francs and deductions of 7,248.00 for reimbursed merchandise transport plus 2,304.20 for fines/incidental receipts. Their sum, 9,552.20, reproduces the stated expense total **442,976.81 francs** after subtraction. The many constituent expense rows have not all been independently transcribed and summed in this audit.

Receipt summary minus that expense total equals **6,080,870.59 francs**. This is an arithmetic remainder under the displayed categories, not an authenticated net-profit figure or proof of capital repayment. Construction additions, reserve allocations, classification differences and complete company accounts need separate reconciliation. The earlier [construction-cost account](EIFFEL-SUPPLY-RECONCILIATION.md) reports company-funded additions; this audit does not assume all are expenses in this operating table.

The [1908 yearbook](EIFFEL-COMPANY-CAPITAL.md) reports five capital repayments of 100 francs per share. Their timing and amounts require original resolutions, payment lists, cancelled coupons or bank entries. Later narrative agreement and sufficient-looking aggregate revenue cannot establish those transactions.
