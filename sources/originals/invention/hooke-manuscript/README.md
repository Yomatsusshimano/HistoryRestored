# CLP/2/13 manuscript delivery images

Source: [Royal Society item](https://makingscience.royalsociety.org/items/clp_2_13/paper-on-telescopes-and-how-to-use-them-by-robert-hooke). Catalog title attributes it tentatively to Robert Hooke and dates it **1667?**. Attribution: **The Royal Society**. Licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). These JPEG delivery images and manifest are preserved unchanged; no claim to master TIFF coverage or physical authentication.

[Original manifest](clp_2_13-manifest.json)

- [Image 1](clp_2_13-image-1.jpg): Fig4 exterior telescope body, eyepiece/dial, slotted screw and tube extension; sideways handwritten description beneath.
- [Image 2](clp_2_13-image-2.jpg): Fig5 support with two posts, crossbars, horizontal screw/crank, central elevation screw and spread legs.
- [Image 3](clp_2_13-image-3.jpg): Composite view: darker cutaway micrometer drawing overlaps lighter exterior/support drawings; paper edges and red marks visible.
- [Image 4](clp_2_13-image-4.jpg): Closer view of cutaway drawing, Fig1 mechanism and Fig3 hair frames; shaped paper edge and black background. Repeated view, not another instrument.
- [Image 5](clp_2_13-image-5.jpg): Lighter exterior/support drawings with overlapping sheet edge at left. Similar designs do not establish different physical instruments.
- [Image 6](clp_2_13-image-6.jpg): Handwritten directions and diagrams for lenses without tubes: suspended objective plate, pole, pulleys/cord and four-thread eyepiece alignment. No calibrated trial.

Six delivery canvases are distinct from the catalog five-page extent. Repeated views are not separate instruments. Hashes, dimensions, comparisons and limits are in the [ledger](../../../../data/hooke-manuscript-crosswalk.json).
