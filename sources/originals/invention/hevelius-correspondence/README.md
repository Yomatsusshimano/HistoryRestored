# Hevelius optical-equipment correspondence

S310: [Oldenburg to Hevelius, 2 August 1669, EL/O2/8](https://makingscience.royalsociety.org/items/el_o2_8/letter-from-henry-oldenburg-to-johannes-hevelius), six delivered surfaces.

S311: [Hevelius to Oldenburg, 29 October 1672, EL/H2/32](https://makingscience.royalsociety.org/items/el_h2_32/letter-from-johannes-hevelius-to-henry-oldenburg-dated-at-danzig-gdansk), four delivered surfaces despite catalog extent of one page.

Both official manifests and all ten unaltered delivered JPEGs are preserved, attributed to **The Royal Society**, under the manifests' [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license. Deliveries use a2400-pixel bounding size and are not archival master TIFFs. All ten surfaces were visually inspected. The [ledger](../../../../data/hevelius-instrument-correspondence.json) records URLs, hashes, selected readings and limitations.

The1669letter reports unfinished telescopic sights; the1672recipient letter renews a request for a small-distance instrument to fit a telescope tube. These equipment identities and the1667commission remain separate. No full transcription, independently reviewed translation, final fulfillment, physical authentication or measured accuracy is claimed.
