# Hevelius to Oldenburg, 21 October 1667

S312, Royal Society Archives EL/H2/14. [Official item](https://makingscience.royalsociety.org/items/el_h2_14/letter-from-johannes-hevelius-to-henry-oldenburg-dated-at-danzig-gdansk).

Four unaltered delivered JPEGs and the official manifest are attributed to **The Royal Society**, under the manifest's [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license. Deliveries have a2400-pixel bounding size and are not master TIFFs. Catalog extent is three pages; three body surfaces and an address side are delivered. All four were visually inspected. The [ledger](../../../../data/hevelius1667-request.json) records hashes, locators and limitations.

The letter requests a long telescope and a description of telescopic sights fitted to sextants. Receipt and reading annotations have separate roles; November14reading agrees with the register witness, while the later compilation prints November4. Neither device identity with the later micrometer nor the printing/reproduction error's cause is established. No full transcription, independent translation review or physical authentication is claimed.
