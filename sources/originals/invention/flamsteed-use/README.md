# Flamsteed reports of micrometer use and redesign

S313: [EL/F1/77, Derby, 2 December 1671](https://makingscience.royalsociety.org/items/el_f1_77/letter-from-john-flamsteed-to-henry-oldenburg-dated-at-derby).

S314: [EL/F1/82, Derby, 5 March 1672 catalog date](https://makingscience.royalsociety.org/items/el_f1_82/letter-from-john-flamsteed-to-henry-oldenburg-dated-at-derby). The manuscript uses a1671/2double-year heading; no Gregorian day/month conversion is supplied.

Four unaltered delivered body/address JPEGs and two official manifests, attributed to **The Royal Society**, under the manifests' [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license. Each catalog extent is one page, with body and address sides delivered. All four surfaces were visually inspected at2400-pixel bounding delivery size; these are not master TIFFs. The [ledger](../../../../data/flamsteed-micrometer-use.json) records hashes, selected readings and limits.

Flamsteed reports practical inconvenience, proposes a redesign and later says it is still unmade. This is qualitative use/fabrication evidence, not calibrated accuracy. No full transcription, comparative trial or physical authentication is claimed.
