# Oldenburg to Hevelius, 9 November 1671

Source S309, Royal Society Archives EL/O2/58. [Official item](https://makingscience.royalsociety.org/items/el_o2_58/letter-from-henry-oldenburg-to-johannes-hevelius-dated-at-london).

Two delivered manuscript JPEGs and the official IIIF manifest, attributed to **The Royal Society**, under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Images are preserved unaltered as delivered at a 2400-pixel bounding size; they are not archival master TIFFs. Both surfaces were visually inspected. The [ledger](../../../../data/oldenburg1671-instrument-delay.json) supplies hashes, image URLs, selected readings and limitations.

The closing paragraph reports delay of Hooke's promised instrument for measuring small distances. The opening discusses a microscope and books separately. Delivery of the delayed instrument and identity with the 1667 commission are unestablished. Corrections and annotations are visible; draft status, handwriting and physical authenticity are not independently authenticated. No full diplomatic transcription or independently reviewed translation is supplied.
